# Software Requirements Specification: Multi-Resort Ski Website and Booking System

**Stakeholder:** Jim (Owner)
**Source:** Requirements elicitation interview (single session, ended early)
**Status:** Draft. Requires validation with the stakeholder (see Section 7, Open Issues).

---

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Purpose
This document states the software requirements for a new, unified website for Jim's business, which operates three ski resorts. It also flags the gaps, ambiguities and contradictions found in the interview transcript.

### 1.2 Background / Current State
- Each of the three ski resorts has its own separate website.
- Room bookings are made only by **fax or telephone**.
- Payments are handled **semi-manually at the resort**.
- Reservation data is not shared across resorts, which creates a risk of **double reservations**.
- No system currently provides weather or snow information.

### 1.3 Scope
The system will replace the three resort websites with a single website. It will provide:
- centralized online room reservations across all three resorts
- online payment
- resort information and availability
- social media promotion
- inventory management
- training class bookings
- customer reviews
- weather and snow condition forecasts

### 1.4 Business Goal
The stakeholder's stated overarching goal is **"happy customers."** The main business driver is eliminating double reservations by centralizing booking data.

## 5. Non-Functional Requirements

| ID | Requirement | Source |
|---|---|---|
| NFR-1 | **Aesthetics:** The website shall have a visually appealing, modern design consistent with the business's brand. Acceptance criteria must be agreed with the stakeholder (e.g., design mock-up approval). | [S] |
| NFR-2 | **Usability:** The website shall be intuitive. A first-time customer should be able to complete a room reservation without assistance. A measurable target (e.g., task completion within N minutes in user testing) is to be agreed. | [S]/[D] |

| NFR-6 | **Availability:** The booking function shall be highly available, especially during peak season. A target uptime is to be agreed. | [D] |
| NFR-7 | **Maintainability:** Non-technical staff shall be able to manage prices, seasons, content, classes and promotions. | [D] |

## 6. Constraints and Assumptions

**Constraints**
- C-1: The system must cover **three ski resorts**.
- C-2: PayPal is the named payment provider (subject to OI-2).

**Assumptions**
- A-1: The three existing websites will be retired once the new site goes live.
- A-2: Telephone and fax bookings may continue during and after the transition.
- A-3: A data provider for weather and snow information is available.

## 7. Open Issues and Clarifications Required

The interview ended early because the stakeholder had to attend to customers, and only F1 was explored in any detail. The following issues need follow-up:

| ID | Issue | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| **OI-1** | **Priority ranking is inconsistent.** The stakeholder's answer "1, 2, 5, 3, 6, 4" was misrecorded by the interviewer, and "Training Class Booking" was dropped from the confirmed list. The confirmation ("Yeees") was hesitant. | Determines release planning and MVP scope. |
| **OI-2** | **Payment method conflict.** The stakeholder first said customers "should wire us the money," then said "PayPal." It is unclear whether both are required, and when payment is due (at booking, as a deposit, or at the resort). | Core to the most critical feature. |
| **OI-3** | **Meaning of "inventory management."** It could mean rooms, rental equipment, retail stock or supplies. | The feature cannot be scoped without this. |
| **OI-4** | **Continuation of phone/fax bookings.** If offline bookings continue, staff must enter them centrally, or double reservations will still occur. | Directly affects the main business goal. |
| **OI-5** | **Social media scope.** Which platforms, and what kind of integration (share buttons, embedded feeds, paid ads, automated posts)? | Effort varies widely. |
| **OI-6** | **Training classes.** What types (ski/snowboard lessons?), who teaches them, capacity, pricing, and whether they can be booked without a room. | The feature cannot be scoped without this. |
| **OI-7** | **Booking policies.** Cancellation and modification rules, refunds, minimum stay, and check-in/out rules. | Required for booking and payment logic. |
| **OI-8** | **Weather/snow access and scope.** "For customers staying at the resort" suggests guests only, while "customers should be able to query" suggests anyone. "And so on" may imply further data such as lift status or avalanche warnings. | Affects access control and data sources. |
| **OI-9** | **Customer accounts.** Do customers need user accounts (e.g., to manage bookings or post reviews), or is guest checkout enough? | Affects F1, F6 and security. |
| **OI-10** | **Migration of existing data.** Do existing reservations from the three current sites or the manual records need to be migrated? | Needed for go-live without double bookings. |
| **OI-11** | **Room types and pricing model.** Are prices set per room or per room type, and are there per-person or per-night rules, packages or discounts? | Required for FR-1.7 to FR-1.9. |
| **OI-12** | **Acceptance criteria for "looks good" and "intuitive."** | NFR-1 and NFR-2 cannot be verified without them. |
| **OI-13** | **Other stakeholders.** Resort managers, front-desk staff and ski instructors were not consulted. | Staff-facing requirements are largely inferred. |
