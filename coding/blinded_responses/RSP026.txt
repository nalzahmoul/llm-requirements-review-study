# Software Requirements: Cool Ski Resorts Online System

## 1. Purpose and scope

The system will support Cool Ski Resorts’ three US resorts through a customer-facing website and centralized business information management. It will enable online reservations, equipment and clothing hire, training-class bookings, and payments, while improving business visibility and reporting.

Implementation must minimize disruption to existing resort operations.

## 2. Users

- **Customers:** View resort services and availability, make bookings and hire arrangements, pay online, and view weather and snow conditions.
- **Resort staff:** Manage customer service, reservations, bookings, hires, and inventory.
- **Accounts staff:** Access booking-related financial information to support accounting and taxation work.
- **Owner/management:** Review operations and reports for individual resorts and the business as a whole.

Detailed access permissions remain to be defined.

## 3. Functional requirements

### 3.1 Resort information and online transactions

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-01 | The website shall present information about each of the three resorts, including available services and contact information. |
| FR-02 | The system shall allow customers to view availability for hotel accommodation, ski equipment and clothing hire, and ski training classes at each resort. |
| FR-03 | The system shall allow customers to reserve hotel accommodation online. |
| FR-04 | The system shall allow customers to arrange ski equipment and clothing hire online. |
| FR-05 | The system shall allow customers to book ski training classes online. |
| FR-06 | The system shall support online payments for reservations, hires, and class bookings. |

### 3.2 Operational data and inventory

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-07 | The system shall maintain a centralized SQL database containing reservations, bookings, hires, and inventory information for all three resorts. |
| FR-08 | The system shall associate operational records with the relevant resort. |
| FR-09 | The system shall enable authorized staff to manage reservations, bookings, hires, and inventory for each resort. |
| FR-10 | The system shall retain booking-related financial information needed by accounts staff. The required financial fields and accounting functions remain to be defined. |
| FR-11 | The system shall support scheduled synchronization between resort data sources and the centralized database. The schedule remains to be agreed; the stakeholder requested infrequent rather than real-time synchronization. |

### 3.3 Reporting

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-12 | The system shall generate occupancy-rate reports. |
| FR-13 | The system shall generate inventory-level reports. |
| FR-14 | The system shall generate booking-trend reports. |
| FR-15 | These reports shall support both individual-resort views and consolidated views across all three resorts. |

### 3.4 Weather and snow conditions

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-16 | The website shall display regional weather forecasts relevant to the resorts. |
| FR-17 | Weather information shall appear on both the main page and during the booking process. |
| FR-18 | The system shall include snow-condition information alongside weather forecasts. |
| FR-19 | Weather and snow-condition information shall update in real time, subject to the selected provider’s capabilities. The acceptable refresh interval remains to be defined. |
| FR-20 | The weather integration shall use the stakeholder’s requested “US weather forecast system.” The exact provider, interface, and availability of snow-condition data require confirmation. |

### 3.5 Social media

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-21 | The website shall provide connections to Cool Ski Resorts’ Twitter, Facebook, and Instagram presences. The form of connection—such as links, embedded feeds, or publishing integration—requires confirmation. |

The stakeholder plans for internal staff to produce weekly social media posts containing resort updates, weather information, and pictures. The system is not required to provide internal content-planning or team-coordination tools.

Automatic sharing, user-generated content, reviews, and testimonials were mentioned together and received a general “yes.” Their individual scope is not sufficiently confirmed to treat them as committed requirements.

## 5. Outstanding decisions and requirements gaps

The following must be resolved before implementation or acceptance criteria can be finalized:

- **Booking rules:** Prices, deposits, cancellations, refunds, booking amendments, hire durations, class capacity, and room-allocation rules.
- **Payments:** Payment methods, provider, transaction handling, and the relationship to existing on-site payments.
- **Synchronization and availability:** Synchronization frequency, direction, conflict handling, and how to prevent double bookings when resort information is not synchronized in real time.
- **Existing systems and migration:** Which local databases will remain in use, what historical data must be migrated, and how telephone and fax bookings will enter the new system.
- **Staff access:** Authentication, roles, permissions, and whether staff access is restricted by resort.
- **Reporting:** Reporting periods, calculations, filters, output formats, and financial-reporting scope.
- **Weather:** Exact US provider, refresh frequency, regional mapping, snow-data source, and behavior when external data is unavailable.
- **Social media:** Required integration depth and whether any automated publishing is needed. The transcript’s ambiguous word “jews,” subsequently interpreted by the interviewer as “news,” should be confirmed as resort news or updates.
- **Operational quality:** Measurable targets for security, privacy, performance, availability, backup, recovery, and accessibility.

These unresolved points are not confirmed requirements and should not be silently assumed.
