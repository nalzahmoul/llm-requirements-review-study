# Software Requirements: Cool Ski Resorts

## 1. Purpose and scope

The system will replace the current static website and semi-manual reservation, booking, equipment-hire, and payment processes with a centralized online platform for all three Cool Ski Resorts locations.

The primary business objectives are to:
- Streamline operations across the three resorts.
- Reach a larger customer base throughout the United States.
- Improve financial reporting.
- Increase competitiveness.
- Introduce the new system with minimal disruption to existing operations.

These are business objectives rather than measurable acceptance criteria; targets remain to be agreed.

## 2. Intended users

| User | Intended use |
|---|---|
| Customers | Register, make reservations, book classes, hire equipment, pay online, and view reservation details. |
| Mary, the owner | Oversee centralized business information and review financial reports. |
| Assistants | Generate reports and potentially help maintain website content. |
| Resort personnel | Operational responsibilities and system access require clarification. |

## 3. Functional requirements

### 3.1 Website and centralized management

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-01 | The system shall provide a customer-facing website for Cool Ski Resorts. |
| FR-02 | The system shall use a centralized database to track reservations, bookings, equipment hires, and inventory for all three resorts. |
| FR-03 | The system shall associate each reservation, booking, equipment hire, and inventory record with the relevant resort. |
| FR-04 | The system shall enable centralized access to business information across the three resorts. |

### 3.2 Reservations, bookings, and equipment hire — highest priority

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-05 | The system shall allow customers to make reservations online for a selected resort. |
| FR-06 | The system shall allow customers to book classes online for a selected resort. |
| FR-07 | The system shall allow customers to arrange equipment hire online for a selected resort. |
| FR-08 | The system shall record online reservations, class bookings, and equipment hires in the centralized database. |
| FR-09 | The equipment-hire process shall integrate with inventory management so customers can reserve available equipment in advance. |

The stakeholder identified online reservations, bookings, and hiring as the highest-priority capabilities. The distinction between a “reservation” and a “booking,” beyond class bookings, requires clarification.

### 3.3 Customer registration and reservation access

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-10 | The system shall support customer registration and login using email. |
| FR-11 | The system shall allow registered customers to view and keep track of their reservations. |
| FR-12 | The system shall make reservation details available in a form customers can present when arriving at the resort. |

The required presentation format—such as an account page, printable confirmation, or booking reference—has not been specified.

### 3.4 Online payments

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-13 | The system shall integrate with a payment gateway to support online payments for reservations, bookings, and equipment hires. |

The payment provider, supported payment methods, payment timing, deposits, refunds, and cancellation rules remain unspecified.

### 3.5 Inventory management

The stakeholder explicitly accepted the proposed inventory capabilities below.

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-14 | The system shall track equipment inventory separately for each resort. |
| FR-15 | The system shall provide real-time visibility of equipment availability. |
| FR-16 | The system shall maintain equipment maintenance schedules. |
| FR-17 | The system shall maintain equipment rental history. |
| FR-18 | The system shall automatically alert relevant users when inventory is low. |
| FR-19 | The system shall automatically alert relevant users when equipment requires servicing. |

Alert recipients, notification channels, low-stock thresholds, and the meaning of “real-time” require definition.

### 3.6 Financial reporting

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-20 | The system shall support generation of financial reports relating to bookings across the three resorts. |
| FR-21 | Financial reports shall include revenue and expenses. |
| FR-22 | The system shall allow Mary and her assistants to access the reporting functionality. |

Report layouts, reporting periods, resort-level breakdowns, export formats, and expense-data sources require clarification. Automated taxation or tax filing was not requested.

## 4. Secondary and provisional capabilities

These items were discussed but are less firmly defined than the core requirements.

| ID | Capability | Status |
|---|---|---|
| PR-01 | Display resort weather information relevant to customers’ stays. | Desired secondary feature; forecast periods, data source, and presentation are unspecified. |
| PR-02 | Support promotion of the website through social media. | Desired outcome; no specific software integration or promotional functionality was defined. |
| PR-03 | Allow assistants to help maintain website content. | Tentative future responsibility; content-management scope and permissions require confirmation. |
| PR-04 | Provide training or support for assistants adopting the new system. | Likely needed; scope and delivery method require confirmation. |

## 5. Non-functional and transition requirements

| ID | Requirement or quality goal |
|---|---|
| NFR-01 | The website shall have a visually appealing presentation. Design acceptance criteria must be agreed with Mary. |
| NFR-02 | The system shall apply standard security measures to protect customer information during registration and login. Specific controls and verification criteria must be defined. |
| NFR-03 | The system shall support centralized operation across all three resorts. |
| TR-01 | Deployment shall be planned to minimize disruption to ongoing resort operations. Acceptable downtime and transition arrangements must be agreed. |

No quantitative requirements were provided for performance, availability, capacity, recovery, or usability.

## 6. Outstanding requirements questions

Before implementation, the following should be resolved:

1. **Reservation scope:** What services or facilities can customers reserve, and how do reservations differ from bookings?
2. **Booking rules:** What are the capacities, schedules, pricing rules, cancellation policies, and modification rules?
3. **Equipment hire:** How are hire periods, equipment categories, returns, damage, and maintenance-related unavailability handled?
4. **Payments:** Which gateway and methods are required, and how should payment failures, deposits, and refunds work?
5. **Reporting:** Where will expense data originate, and which calculations, filters, and output formats are required?
6. **Access control:** What may Mary, assistants, and resort personnel view or change?
7. **Existing data:** Must local databases be migrated or integrated, and how will existing reservations remain operational during transition?
8. **Secondary features:** What weather, social-media, and website-maintenance functionality is actually required?
9. **Acceptance criteria:** What measurable targets will demonstrate improved operations and a minimally disruptive deployment?
