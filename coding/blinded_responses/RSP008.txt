# Software Requirements Specification: Ski Resort Customer Management & Booking Website

## 1. Purpose and Context

**Stakeholder:** Owner of three ski resorts.

**Current situation:** All customer management is manual. Reservations arrive by phone and fax, and payment is handled in person at the resort.

**Project goal:** A website through which customers can book rooms, equipment, and ski training classes and pay online. The website must also attract new customers through social media and give the owner centralized control over customers, reservations, payments, and equipment inventory.

**Stakeholder's stated priority:** The reservation and payment system.

### Source Legend
Each requirement is tagged with how it was obtained from the interview:
- **[S] Stated:** The stakeholder said it directly.
- **[C] Confirmed:** The interviewer proposed it and the stakeholder agreed, often with only a "Yes." These need validation.
- **[D] Derived:** The reviewer inferred it as necessary to fulfil a stated need. It was not discussed and needs validation.

### Priority Legend (MoSCoW)
- **Must:** Essential.
- **Should:** Important but not critical.
- **Could:** Desirable.

## 5. Out of Scope

- **Customer reviews and feedback:** The stakeholder explicitly declined this. Social media is intended for advertising only.

---

## 6. Assumptions

1. A single website serves all three resorts.
2. The stakeholder prefers an online-first process that replaces phone and fax bookings, though staff may still enter bookings manually.
3. Weather data and payment processing will use third-party services.
4. "Inventory" refers to rental ski equipment only.

---

## 7. Open Issues and Questions for Follow-Up

The interview was short, and many answers were single-word confirmations. The following should be clarified before design begins:

1. **Booking flow:** What steps should a typical booking follow? The interviewer asked for a scenario, but the stakeholder did not provide one.
2. **Pay on site:** Should paying at the resort remain an option, or must all bookings be prepaid online?
3. **Payment methods:** Is credit card plus PayPal the final list? Are deposits or partial payments needed?
4. **Cancellation and refund policy:** What are the rules and deadlines?
5. **Customer accounts:** Must customers register, or should guest checkout be allowed?
6. **Customer management:** What does "manage my customers" include beyond bookings, e.g., customer history, newsletters, or marketing emails?
7. **Classes:** How are ski training classes structured, e.g., group or private, skill levels, instructor assignment, capacity?
8. **Equipment:** What equipment types and sizes need tracking? Should customers select sizes when booking?
9. **Rooms:** What room types exist, and how are rooms priced, e.g., seasonal rates?
10. **Languages and currency:** The stakeholder appears to be German-speaking. Is the website needed in multiple languages, and in which currencies?
11. **Social media:** Which specific platforms are wanted, and who maintains the social media content?
12. **Weather:** Should the forecast include snow and slope conditions in addition to general weather?
13. **Staff needs:** Which staff roles will use the system? Their input should be gathered.
14. **Constraints:** What are the budget, timeline, hosting preferences, and any existing systems (e.g., accounting) to integrate with?
