# Software Requirements: Ski Resort Website

## 1. Purpose and Scope

The system shall provide a customer-facing website serving Jim’s three ski resorts, with centralized reservation data to prevent double bookings.

The business goals are to improve customer satisfaction, enable online room reservations, and help customers explore resort offerings and conditions.

## 2. Functional Requirements

### 2.1 Centralized Room Reservations — Highest Priority

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-01 | The system shall centralize room availability and reservation data for all three resorts. |
| FR-02 | The system shall allow customers to view available rooms at each resort. |
| FR-03 | The system shall allow customers to select and reserve a room online. |
| FR-04 | The system shall prevent conflicting reservations for the same room over overlapping stay dates. |
| FR-05 | The system shall display room prices and differences in pricing between seasons. |

### 2.2 Payments

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-06 | The system shall integrate with PayPal to process customer payments. |
| FR-07 | The system shall provide payment confirmations. |
| FR-08 | The system shall automatically issue payment receipts. |

**Clarification needed:** The stakeholder initially requested payment by bank transfer, then specified PayPal and confirmed the PayPal-based summary. Whether bank transfers must also be supported remains unresolved.

### 2.3 Resort Information and Promotion

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-09 | The website shall allow customers to view the offerings available at each of the three resorts. |
| FR-10 | The system shall support promotion and advertising through social media integration. |

The specific social media platforms and integration capabilities have not been defined.

### 2.4 Weather and Snow Conditions

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-11 | The system shall allow customers to access weather information and forecasts relevant to the resorts. |
| FR-12 | The system shall allow customers to access snow-condition information and forecasts relevant to the resorts. |

Data sources, update frequency, and forecast periods require clarification.

### 2.5 Inventory Management

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-13 | The system shall provide inventory management functionality. |

The inventory types, users, and required management operations have not been specified.

### 2.6 Training Class Booking

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-14 | The system shall allow customers to book training classes. |

Class types, schedules, capacity limits, pricing, and payment rules require clarification.

### 2.7 Customer Feedback

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-15 | The system shall provide functionality for customer feedback or reviews. |

The format, publication rules, and moderation requirements have not been defined.

## 3. User Experience Requirements

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| UX-01 | The website shall have an aesthetically pleasing visual design. |
| UX-02 | The website shall provide an intuitive customer interface for discovering resort offerings and completing reservations. |

These are confirmed stakeholder expectations, but measurable acceptance criteria must be agreed upon.

## 4. Feature Priorities

The stakeholder explicitly confirmed the following ranking presented by the interviewer:

1. Centralized booking and prevention of double reservations
2. Aesthetically pleasing and intuitive website
3. Inventory management
4. Social media promotion and advertising integration
5. Customer feedback or reviews
6. Weather and snow-condition forecasts

**Priority discrepancy:** Training class booking was requested but omitted from the confirmed ranking. Its relative priority must be clarified.

## 5. Outstanding Requirements Questions

Before implementation, the following matters need resolution:

- Whether the new website replaces all three existing websites or whether they remain connected to the centralized reservation system.
- Whether telephone and fax reservations will continue and how those bookings will enter the centralized system.
- When a reservation becomes confirmed, particularly in relation to payment, and how unpaid or failed-payment bookings are handled.
- Whether cancellation, modification, refund, deposit, or partial-payment functionality is required.
- How seasons and seasonal room prices are defined and maintained.
- Which staff roles need access to manage rooms, reservations, prices, inventory, classes, and reviews.
- The detailed scope of inventory, class booking, social media, and feedback functionality.
- Measurable usability, performance, availability, security, and privacy requirements.

These items were not established in the interview and should not be treated as confirmed functionality.
