# Software Requirements Specification: Cool Ski Resorts Digital Management System

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Purpose
This document specifies the requirements for a system that digitalizes the operations of Cool Ski Resorts. The requirements come from an elicitation interview with the owner, Mary Smith. Each requirement is labeled with its source:

- **[S]** Stated directly by the stakeholder.
- **[D]** Derived or inferred by the reviewer from stated needs. These need stakeholder confirmation.

### 1.2 Business Context
Cool Ski Resorts operates three ski resorts in different mountain locations in the US. Each resort offers:

- hotel room bookings,
- ski equipment and clothing hire, and
- ski training classes.

**Current state:**
- Reservations are taken by telephone or fax.
- Bookings are handled semi-manually in a local database at each resort. Resorts do not share data.
- Financial records are maintained by assistants.

### 1.3 Business Objectives
| ID | Objective | Source |
|---|---|---|
| BO-1 | Digitalize all resort operations. | [S] |
| BO-2 | Carry out the transition with minimal disruption to ongoing operations. | [S] |
| BO-3 | Make data from every resort accessible at every resort. | [S] |
| BO-4 | Let customers book services online. | [S] |
| BO-5 | Improve the company's online presence and promotion. | [S] |

### 1.4 Scope and Priorities
The stakeholder ranked the features as follows:

| Priority | Feature |
|---|---|
| **High** | Online customer portal |
| **High** | Centralized, cross-resort data access |
| **High** | Digitalization of operations |
| **Lower** | Customer website design |
| **Lower** | Weather forecast |
| **Lower** | Social media promotion |

The requirements below use MoSCoW priorities: Must, Should, Could, Won't.

## 3. Functional Requirements

## 6. Assumptions

1. "Viewing is fine for everybody" refers to all staff, not customers or the public.
2. A Resort Lead's edit rights apply to their own resort only. The phrase "lead of each resort" suggests this, but it was not confirmed.
3. "Bookings" covers hotel reservations, class bookings, and equipment hire reservations.
4. Customers will pay online through the portal.
5. Customers may still contact resorts by telephone, and staff will enter those bookings into the new system.
6. The weather forecast will come from a third-party weather service.

---

## 7. Open Issues and Questions for the Stakeholder

The interview left several areas undefined. These should be resolved before design begins:

1. **Access scope:** Can Resort Leads edit data from other resorts? Can Receptionists edit bookings at any resort or only their own? What rights does the Owner have?
2. **Receptionist rights:** Can Receptionists record payments and refunds, or only bookings?
3. **Payments:** Is payment taken at booking, as a deposit, or on arrival? Which payment methods are needed? What is the refund and cancellation policy?
4. **Reporting:** Beyond payment data, which reports are needed (e.g., occupancy, revenue per resort, equipment utilization)?
5. **Financial integration:** Do the finance assistants use accounting software the system must integrate with?
6. **Legacy data:** How much historical data exists in the local databases, and must all of it be migrated?
7. **Fax and phone:** Should phone and fax reservations be phased out completely, or kept as staff-assisted channels?
8. **Social media:** Which platforms? Is the goal passive integration (links and sharing) or active marketing features such as promotions and campaigns?
9. **Additional features:** Are loyalty programs, discounts or packages, multiple languages, or multiple currencies needed?
10. **Constraints:** What are the budget, timeline, and preferred launch date (e.g., before the next ski season)?
11. **Other stakeholders:** Resort Leads, receptionists, rental staff, instructors, and finance assistants were not interviewed. Interviewing them is strongly recommended, because they will use the internal system daily and will be most affected by the transition.

---

## 8. Reviewer Notes on the Elicitation

- The stakeholder provided clear high-level goals and priorities but delegated most detailed decisions. Many requirements here are therefore derived and should be validated, ideally with mock-ups or prototypes of the customer portal and staff screens.
- The stakeholder did not elaborate on the lower-priority features (website design, weather, social media). Those requirements are kept intentionally broad.
- Non-functional requirements, especially security, privacy, and payment compliance, were not discussed. They are added here as necessary for a system that handles customer and payment data.
