# Software Requirements: Cool Ski Resorts Online System

## 1. Purpose and scope

Cool Ski Resorts requires a web-based system serving its three U.S.-based ski resorts. The system will replace or streamline manual reservation and payment processes, centralize operational data, and enable customers to book and pay for services online.

The business objectives are to:
- Improve customer experience through convenient self-service.
- Reduce manual work, errors, and coordination effort between resorts.
- Increase customer reach and online visibility.
- Improve operational efficiency while minimizing disruption to current operations.

## 2. Users

- **Customers:** Book accommodation, equipment rentals, and training classes; pay online; and view resort information.
- **Resort staff:** Manage reservations, inventory, customer information, and payment records, and support guest arrival.
- **Owners and management:** Monitor operations and financial information across the three resorts.

Detailed permissions for these user groups remain to be defined.

## 3. Functional requirements

### 3.1 Online reservations and bookings — Priority 1

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-01 | The system shall allow customers to book hotel rooms online at the three resorts. |
| FR-02 | The system shall allow customers to reserve ski equipment rentals online. |
| FR-03 | The system shall allow customers to register for training classes online. |
| FR-04 | The system shall provide self-service online booking at any time of day. |
| FR-05 | The system shall send customers a booking confirmation by email after a booking is made. |
| FR-06 | The booking confirmation shall include a booking number, a QR code, or both, to identify the reservation and simplify guest arrival. The identifier format remains to be confirmed. |

**Check-in requirement requiring confirmation:** The stakeholder expressed a desire for staff to scan booking QR codes using phones to reduce the guest entry process. The interviewer’s final interpretation was not explicitly confirmed. Whether QR scanning is required, whether a booking number is sufficient, and what scanning should do must be clarified.

### 3.2 Centralized database — Priority 2

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-07 | The system shall use a centralized database shared across all three resorts. |
| FR-08 | The system shall centrally maintain reservation, customer, inventory, and payment/financial information. |
| FR-09 | The system shall allow authorized staff to manage reservations and customer information for resort operations. |
| FR-10 | The system shall make current reservation, inventory, and financial information available across the resorts to support coordination and monitoring. |

### 3.3 Integrated online payments — Priority 3

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-11 | The system shall allow customers to pay online for booked services. |
| FR-12 | The system shall support booking deposits and full payments. |
| FR-13 | The system shall record payments against their associated bookings in the centralized database. |

Payment methods, deposit rules, balance-payment timing, and the payment provider were not specified.

### 3.4 Inventory and resource management — Priority 4

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-14 | The system shall track ski equipment availability in real time. |
| FR-15 | The system shall track room occupancy and availability in real time. |
| FR-16 | The system shall track other bookable resource availability as those resources are defined. |
| FR-17 | The system shall update availability when bookings affect resource capacity. |
| FR-18 | The system shall prevent bookings that exceed available room or resource capacity. |

Although full inventory management is ranked fourth, sufficient availability control is needed when online booking is introduced to prevent overbooking.

### 3.5 Social media integration — Priority 5

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-19 | The website shall provide links to the resorts’ Facebook, Instagram, and Twitter accounts to support promotion and customer interaction. |

Social feeds, automated posting, social login, and other deeper integrations were not requested explicitly.

### 3.6 Weather and snow information — Priority 6

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-20 | The website shall display current weather and snow conditions relevant to the resorts. |
| FR-21 | The website shall display weather and snow forecasts to help guests plan their trips. |

The information provider, update frequency, and handling of unavailable data remain to be defined.

## 4. Nonfunctional and transition requirements

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| NFR-01 | The booking and payment processes shall be simple and easy to use, avoiding unnecessary complexity. Measurable usability criteria must be agreed. |
| NFR-02 | The system shall process online payments securely. Specific security controls and applicable compliance requirements must be established before implementation. |
| NFR-03 | The system shall support 24/7 customer self-service. Availability targets and maintenance arrangements remain to be agreed. |
| NFR-04 | Operational data shall be updated sufficiently promptly to support real-time availability tracking and coordination. Maximum acceptable update delays remain to be defined. |
| NFR-05 | The system design shall accommodate later addition of customer reviews, loyalty functionality, and mobile optimization without treating these as committed initial-release features. |
| TR-01 | The transition from the existing resort-local processes and databases shall minimize disruption to ongoing resort operations. Migration and rollout arrangements must be agreed. |

## 5. Future enhancements—not committed to the initial release

The stakeholder requested advance planning for:

- **Customer reviews and testimonials:** Allow guests to leave feedback for prospective customers.
- **Loyalty program:** Allow repeat customers to enroll for rewards or discounts.
- **Mobile optimization:** Provide a fully responsive website that is easy to use on mobile devices.

These enhancements are lower priority than the six core capabilities.

## 6. Outstanding requirements decisions

The following topics were not resolved in the interview:

1. Booking rules, including dates, party sizes, room selection, equipment quantities, and class capacities.
2. Whether customers need accounts or may book as guests.
3. Cancellation, amendment, refund, and no-show policies.
4. Pricing, taxes, deposit amounts, and remaining-balance payment rules.
5. Booking-number versus QR-code requirements and the exact arrival/check-in workflow.
6. Staff roles, access permissions, and cross-resort data access.
7. Scope of financial monitoring, taxation support, and reporting.
8. Migration of existing data and continued handling of phone, fax, and on-site bookings.
9. Security, privacy, performance, accessibility, and availability acceptance criteria.
10. External service providers and integration details for payments, email, and weather data.

These decisions should be confirmed before the requirements are treated as a complete implementation baseline.
