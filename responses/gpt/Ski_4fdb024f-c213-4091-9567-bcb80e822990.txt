# Software Requirements: Cool Ski Resorts

## 1. Purpose and Scope

The system shall digitalize the operations of Cool Ski Resorts, a chain of three ski resorts in the United States. It shall support hotel reservations, ski equipment and clothing hire, ski class bookings, inventory management, and financial tracking.

The system shall provide an online customer portal and shared access to data across all three resorts. Implementation shall be phased to avoid disrupting ongoing operations.

## 2. Users

- **Customers:** Book and manage resort services through the customer portal.
- **Resort staff:** View resort data.
- **Resort leads:** View and edit operational data, subject to the resort-level scope to be confirmed.
- **Receptionists:** View data and edit bookings.
- **Owner and financial assistants:** Require access to operational and financial information; their specific permissions remain to be confirmed.

## 3. Functional Requirements

### 3.1 Customer Portal

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-01 | The system shall provide an online customer portal for the three resorts. |
| FR-02 | The portal shall allow customers to make hotel reservations at a selected resort. |
| FR-03 | The portal shall allow customers to book ski training classes at a selected resort. |
| FR-04 | The portal shall support booking ski equipment and clothing hire at a selected resort. |
| FR-05 | The portal shall allow customers to manage their reservations. The permitted changes and cancellation rules require clarification. |

### 3.2 Resort Operations

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-06 | The system shall maintain hotel reservation records for each resort. |
| FR-07 | The system shall support digital management of ski training classes and their bookings. |
| FR-08 | The system shall maintain inventory records for ski equipment and clothing at each resort. |
| FR-09 | The system shall maintain equipment and clothing hire records for each resort. |
| FR-10 | The system shall support financial tracking, including recording and viewing payment data associated with resort operations. |

### 3.3 Shared Data and Updates

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-11 | The system shall provide a shared database containing reservation, inventory, class booking, hire, and payment data for all three resorts. |
| FR-12 | The system shall associate operational records with the relevant resort. |
| FR-13 | Authorized staff at any resort shall be able to access data for all three resorts. |
| FR-14 | Changes to shared data shall be reflected live across resort locations. The maximum acceptable update delay requires definition. |

### 3.4 Access Control

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-15 | The system shall enforce access permissions based on staff roles. |
| FR-16 | All resort staff shall have permission to view shared resort data. Any restrictions on sensitive customer or payment information require confirmation. |
| FR-17 | Resort leads shall have permission to edit operational records. Whether this permission applies only to their own resort or across all resorts requires confirmation. |
| FR-18 | Receptionists shall have permission to edit booking records. |
| FR-19 | Staff who are neither resort leads nor receptionists shall have view-only access. Receptionists’ editing rights shall be limited to bookings. |

### 3.5 Website and Promotion

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-20 | The system shall provide a customer-facing website with a visually appealing design. Design acceptance criteria shall be agreed with the stakeholder. |
| FR-21 | The website shall display weather forecasts relevant to the resort locations. |
| FR-22 | The solution shall support promotion through social media. The required platforms and promotional capabilities require further elicitation. |

## 4. Quality and Transition Requirements

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| QR-01 | The customer portal shall be easy for customers to use when booking and managing services. Measurable usability criteria require agreement. |
| QR-02 | The system shall support access to shared operational data from each of the three resorts. |
| TR-01 | The system shall be introduced through phased implementation. |
| TR-02 | The transition shall preserve continuity of resort operations and avoid disruption to existing services. Cutover arrangements and acceptance criteria require agreement. |
| TR-03 | The implementation shall replace the current semi-manual workflows and local-database approach for the operations identified in scope. |

## 5. Priorities

**Highest priority, as identified by the stakeholder:**
1. Customer portal.
2. Cross-resort access to live data.
3. Digitalization of resort operations.

**Additional requested capabilities:**
- Attractive customer-facing website.
- Weather forecasts.
- Social media promotion support.

The stakeholder did not specify a relative implementation order among these additional capabilities.

## 6. Outstanding Clarifications

The following details are not established by the interview and should not be treated as agreed requirements:

- Booking workflows, required customer information, availability checks, and overbooking prevention.
- Reservation modification, cancellation, refund, and no-show policies.
- Equipment hire availability, collection, return, and stock-management rules.
- Ski class schedules, capacities, and instructor management.
- Whether the system must process online payments or only record payment information.
- Required financial reports and accounting integrations.
- Customer identification or account requirements.
- Owner and financial-assistant permissions, and the resort scope of editing rights.
- Privacy, security, and payment-data access restrictions.
- Required response times, availability targets, and the meaning of “live” updates.
- Migration of existing local database records and treatment of telephone and fax bookings during transition.
- Weather data sources and social media functionality.
- Implementation phases and measurable criteria for a disruption-free transition.
