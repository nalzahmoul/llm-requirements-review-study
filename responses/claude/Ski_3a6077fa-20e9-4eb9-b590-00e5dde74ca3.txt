# Software Requirements Specification: Resort Online Management System

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Purpose
This document states the software requirements for an online management system for a group of three ski resorts. It is based on a single elicitation interview with one stakeholder, the resort staff member responsible for implementing the system.

### 1.2 Background and Current State
- All three resorts take reservations and equipment hire by **telephone and fax**.
- Payments are handled **semi-manually** at each resort and stored in a **separate local database per resort**.
- Assistants manage accounts, taxation, and the financial side of bookings for each resort.
- Coordinating the three resorts is becoming increasingly burdensome.

### 1.3 Business Goals
| ID | Goal |
|----|------|
| BG-1 | Reduce the staff effort needed for reservations, bookings, and payments ("less staff"). |
| BG-2 | Make the booking process more transparent. |
| BG-3 | Streamline coordination across the three resorts. |
| BG-4 | Increase the resorts' visibility and promotion through social media. |
| BG-5 | Strengthen the brand, including a new logo. |

### 1.4 Stakeholders and Users
- **Resort management and implementation lead** (the interviewee).
- **Resort staff and assistants** handling accounts, taxation, bookings, and inventory.
- **Customers** booking stays, rentals, and training classes.

*Note:* The stakeholder named no other stakeholders. This is a risk (see Section 7).

### 1.5 Feature Priority (agreed in interview)
1. Website
2. Online reservations and bookings
3. Payment processing
4. Social media integration
5. Weather and snow forecasts
6. Inventory management
7. Customer feedback (Google Reviews)
8. Logo design (can run alongside the other features)

## 2. Functional Requirements

Each requirement is labelled by source:
- **[S]** stated directly by the stakeholder
- **[C]** proposed by the interviewer and confirmed by the stakeholder
- **[D]** derived or implied by the reviewer and needing validation

### 2.1 Public Website (Priority 1)
| ID | Requirement | Source |
|----|-------------|--------|
| FR-WEB-1 | The system shall provide a public website presenting the offerings of each of the three resorts. | S |
| FR-WEB-2 | The website shall show customers what is available at each resort, including accommodation, rentals, and training classes. | S |
| FR-WEB-3 | The website shall show clearly labelled buttons that distinguish (a) where to find information about the offer and (b) where to book. | S |
| FR-WEB-4 | The website shall use a ski-themed visual design. | S |
| FR-WEB-5 | The website shall show the resort's new logo once it is available. | D |

### 2.2 Online Reservations and Bookings (Priority 2)
| ID | Requirement | Source |
|----|-------------|--------|
| FR-BK-1 | The system shall allow customers to reserve hotel/hostel accommodation online at any of the three resorts. | S |
| FR-BK-2 | The system shall allow customers to book equipment rentals ("hire") online. | S |
| FR-BK-3 | The system shall allow customers to book training classes (e.g., ski lessons) online. | S |
| FR-BK-4 | The booking process shall be a step-by-step (wizard-style) flow. | S |
| FR-BK-5 | The booking flow shall let customers add optional add-ons and customizations, including at least: flights, breakfast, accommodation-only (no ski pass), bus tickets, restaurant vouchers, and insurance. | S |
| FR-BK-6 | Bookings shall be confirmed instantly, without manual staff processing. | S |
| FR-BK-7 | The system shall show real-time availability so customers know what can be booked before they commit. | D (supports BG-2 transparency) |
| FR-BK-8 | The system shall send the customer a booking confirmation with the full itemized booking and price. | D (supports BG-2) |
| FR-BK-9 | The system shall keep all three resorts' bookings in a single central system, replacing the separate local databases. | D (supports BG-3) |
| FR-BK-10 | Resort staff shall be able to view and manage bookings for all resorts through a back-office interface. | D |

### 2.3 Payment Processing (Priority 3)
| ID | Requirement | Source |
|----|-------------|--------|
| FR-PAY-1 | The system shall accept online payment at the time of booking. | S |
| FR-PAY-2 | The system shall support as many payment methods as practical, including at least credit/debit cards and PayPal. | S (minimum set to be confirmed) |

| FR-PAY-4 | The system shall give assistants financial records and reports that support accounting and taxation for each resort. | D (based on current assistant duties) |

| ID | Requirement | Source |

## 3. Non-Functional Requirements

| ID | Category | Requirement | Source |
|----|----------|-------------|--------|
| NFR-1 | Usability | The website and booking flow shall be intuitive for customers with no prior instruction. | S |
| NFR-2 | Aesthetics | The website shall be visually appealing and consistently ski-themed. | S |
| NFR-3 | Performance | Booking confirmation shall be returned instantly (target to be defined, e.g., within a few seconds of payment). | S |
| NFR-4 | Efficiency | The system shall reduce manual staff effort for reservations, payments, and coordination. | S (success metric to be defined) |
| NFR-5 | Transparency | Prices, availability, add-ons, and booking status shall be clearly visible to customers. | S |
| NFR-6 | Security | Payment processing shall comply with applicable payment card security standards (e.g., PCI DSS). | D |
| NFR-7 | Privacy | Customer personal data shall be handled under applicable data protection law (e.g., GDPR if the resorts operate in the EU). | D |
| NFR-8 | Availability | The booking website should be available 24/7, since it replaces phone and fax booking. | D |
| NFR-9 | Compatibility | The website should work on desktop and mobile devices. | D |

## 5. Constraints and Assumptions
- **A-1:** The system will serve all three resorts from one shared platform.
- **A-2:** Existing data in each resort's local database will need to be migrated.
- **A-3:** "Hire" refers to equipment rental, and "training classes" refers to ski/snowboard lessons.
- **A-4:** "Hostel" accommodation is offered alongside or in place of hotel accommodation.
- **C-1:** The stakeholder has limited time for elaboration, so requirements must be validated in short review sessions.

---

## 6. Out of Scope (unless confirmed otherwise)
- Full accounting and tax software. The system provides financial data, but filing and accounting may remain with the assistants or existing tools.
- Staff payroll and HR.

---

## 7. Open Issues and Questions for Follow-up

**Several key requirements are vague or unconfirmed and should be resolved before design begins:**

1. **Payment methods:** "As many as possible" needs a confirmed minimum list and any target customer regions or currencies.
2. **Add-on fulfilment:** Are flights, bus tickets, and insurance sold by the resort, by partners, or through third-party integrations or redirects?
3. **Weather provider:** Which specific local services count as "the best"? Are there licensing costs?
4. **Social media scope:** Should the system only link to profiles, or also schedule and post content and run promotions?
5. **Back-office needs:** What do staff and assistants need, such as reports, roles, and permissions? Who will use the system internally?
6. **Cancellations and refunds:** What are the policies, and should they be handled online?
7. **Languages and currencies:** Do international customers require these?
8. **Data migration:** How should historical bookings and payments move from the local databases?
9. **Success metrics:** How will "less staff" and "more transparency" be measured?
10. **Inventory detail:** Is maintenance tracking, damage reporting, or reordering of supplies needed?
11. **Additional stakeholders:** Resort assistants, front-desk staff, and instructors have not been consulted, though they will be directly affected. Interviewing them is strongly recommended.
12. **Budget and timeline:** Neither was discussed.
