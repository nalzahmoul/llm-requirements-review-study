# Software Requirements: Cool Ski Resorts Online Reservation & Management System

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Purpose
This document states the software requirements for a new online system for Cool Ski Resorts. The requirements come from an elicitation interview with Jim, the owner. The interview ended early at the stakeholder's request, so several areas are incomplete. These are listed in Section 8.

### 1.2 Business Context
Cool Ski Resorts runs **three resorts**. Today it operates as follows:
- Hotel reservations, equipment hire, and class bookings are handled semi-manually by **telephone and fax**.
- Each resort keeps its **own local database**, with little coordination between resorts.
- Payments are **processed manually on-site** by staff.
- Inventory is **tracked manually and separately** at each resort.

These problems follow from the current setup:
- Staff workload is high in accounts, customer service, and inventory.
- Manual records lead to errors.
- No one has an overall view of inventory.
- The business has limited visibility and reach to new customers.

### 1.3 Business Goals
- **BG-1:** Let customers book comfortably from home without calling or waiting on the phone.
- **BG-2:** Streamline operations and improve coordination across the three resorts.
- **BG-3:** Increase visibility and reach a larger customer base.
- **BG-4:** Reduce manual workload and errors for staff.

### 1.4 Notation
- **[S]**: The stakeholder stated this requirement directly.
- **[I]**: The requirement is inferred from the interview or is standard good practice. It needs stakeholder confirmation.
- **Priority:** M = Must have, S = Should have, C = Could have (MoSCoW).

## 6. Constraints and Priorities

- **Priority order:**
  1. Online hotel reservation, equipment hire, and class booking, with online payment. This is the highest priority.
  2. Inventory management (centralized, real-time, dashboard).
  3. Customer website (in practice, a prerequisite for item 1).
  4. Social media integration and weather/snow conditions.
  5. Online customer support / chat.

  Only the top priority was explicitly ranked by the stakeholder. The rest of the order is a proposal and needs confirmation.
- **Timeline:** The stakeholder wants the booking system "tomorrow," which signals high urgency. A realistic schedule must be agreed. A phased delivery that puts booking and payment first is recommended.
- **Scope:** The stakeholder said no further features are needed "for now." Any additions should go through change control.

---

## 7. Assumptions

- A-1: All three resorts will adopt the new centralized system and stop using their separate local processes.
- A-2: Staff will have internet-connected devices to update inventory in real time.
- A-3: Third-party services (payment, weather, social media) will be used rather than built in-house.
