# Software Requirements: Cool Ski Resorts

## 1. Purpose and scope

Cool Ski Resorts operates three ski resorts in different mountain locations in the United States. Its services include resort hotels, ski equipment rental, and ski training.

The proposed solution will replace the current fax- and telephone-based booking and administration processes with a new digital system supported by a central database.

### Business objectives
- Provide guests with a pleasant, seamless stay.
- Digitize booking and administrative operations across the three resorts.
- Increase company visibility and attract more customers through a separate social-media initiative.

## 2. Functional requirements

| ID | Requirement | Qualification |
|---|---|---|
| FR-01 | The system shall support booking and administrative operations for all three Cool Ski Resorts locations. | Derived from the company-wide digitalization scope; detailed workflows require confirmation. |
| FR-02 | The system shall allow customers to make reservations online. | Explicitly requested. |
| FR-03 | The system shall allow customers to make payments online. | Explicitly requested. |
| FR-04 | The system shall provide digital inventory management covering all resort inventory. | Explicitly requested; inventory categories and operations remain undefined. |
| FR-05 | The system shall use a central database to support booking, administration, and inventory management across the resorts. | Central database explicitly requested; detailed data scope requires confirmation. |
| FR-06 | The system shall provide digital workflows that allow booking and administrative activities to be completed without relying on fax or telephone. | Explicitly requested; activities must be identified in follow-up elicitation. |

**Service coverage to confirm:** Hotels, ski rentals, and ski training are the business’s stated services. The stakeholder did not explicitly confirm which of these must support online reservations and payments at launch.

## 4. Training and rollout requirements

These are delivery requirements rather than software features.

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|

Training content, duration, participating roles, and scheduling remain to be defined.

## 5. Related social-media initiative

The following requirements concern marketing services and do not establish software functionality:

- Engage an external social-media agency.
- Require the agency to train internal staff.
- Prioritize agency expertise; agency size is not important.
- Favor innovative approaches.
- Implement the social-media initiative and associated training within the year of the interview. The calendar year is unspecified.

No specific social-media platform was selected. The transcript does not establish a requirement for integration between social media and the new booking system.
