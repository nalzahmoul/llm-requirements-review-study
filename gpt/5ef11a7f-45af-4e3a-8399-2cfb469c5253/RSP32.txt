# Software Requirements: Cool Ski Resorts

## 1. Purpose and scope

The system will modernize Cool Ski Resorts’ online presence and consolidate booking, rental, payment, and reporting operations for its three US resorts.

The primary objectives are to:
- Enable customers to find information and book services online.
- Increase customer acquisition through promotions and partnerships.
- Automate routine work while allowing staff to handle exceptions and manual transactions.
- Provide centralized operational and financial information.
- Introduce the system without interrupting day-to-day business.

Initial operational scope covers the three existing resorts and their current services: accommodation, ski equipment and clothing rentals, and ski training classes.

## 2. Users

- **Customers:** Browse resort information, book services, make payments, and submit feedback.
- **Resort staff:** Manage bookings and rentals, handle walk-ins and exceptions, and respond to feedback.
- **Accounting staff:** Manage accounting records and produce financial reports.
- **Owner and management:** Review cross-resort performance, manage promotions, and access reports.

Specific permissions for these users require further definition.

## 3. Functional requirements

### 3.1 Website and resort information

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| WEB-01 | The system shall provide a customer-facing website covering all three resorts. |
| WEB-02 | The website shall display resort information, available services, and contact information. |
| WEB-03 | The website shall display photographs of the resorts and their facilities. |
| WEB-04 | The website shall provide an events calendar for resort events and activities. |
| WEB-05 | The system shall display current weather and snow conditions for each resort through an external weather service. |
| WEB-06 | The system shall display real-time ski lift operating status and wait times, subject to the availability of suitable data sources. |

### 3.2 Bookings and rentals

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| BKG-01 | Customers shall be able to book accommodation online at any of the three resorts. |
| BKG-02 | Customers shall be able to reserve ski equipment and clothing rentals online. |
| BKG-03 | Customers shall be able to book ski training classes online. |
| BKG-04 | The system shall maintain booking and rental records for all resorts in one centralized database, with each record associated with the relevant resort. |
| BKG-05 | The system shall automatically process routine reservations that do not require staff intervention. |
| BKG-06 | The system shall identify reservation-processing problems and make affected reservations available for staff review. |
| BKG-07 | The system shall track equipment and clothing inventory, including quantities available and currently rented out. |
| BKG-08 | Authorized staff shall be able to manually add, correct, and remove booking and rental records. |
| BKG-09 | Staff shall be able to record walk-in bookings and rentals without requiring customers to use the online booking process. |

### 3.3 Payments and accounting

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| PAY-01 | The system shall support online payment for bookable services using commonly used payment options. The specific payment methods shall be agreed before implementation. |
| PAY-02 | The system shall maintain accounting information associated with business transactions across all resorts. |
| PAY-03 | Authorized staff shall be able to manually add, correct, and remove accounting records. |
| PAY-04 | The system shall automatically generate accounting reports at configured times. |
| PAY-05 | Authorized users shall be able to generate accounting reports on demand. |

Manual removal of accounting records is a stakeholder request; its implementation must be reconciled with applicable record-retention and audit obligations.

### 3.4 Operational reporting

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| RPT-01 | The system shall generate booking, rental, and accounting reports for an individual resort. |
| RPT-02 | The system shall generate consolidated booking, rental, and accounting reports covering all three resorts. |

### 3.5 Promotions and partnerships

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| MKT-01 | The system shall support running promotions and advertisements through connected social media platforms. |
| MKT-02 | The system shall support promotional activities conducted with travel agencies and tourism networks. |
| MKT-03 | The system shall provide promotion-performance insights, including changes in booking volume, campaign reach, and audience reception, where supporting data is available. |
| MKT-04 | The system shall allow users to compare booking volumes during promotional and non-promotional periods. |
| MKT-05 | Authorized users shall be able to define promotion-report templates. |
| MKT-06 | The system shall automatically generate a report using the specified template after a promotion ends. |

The specific social platforms, partner interfaces, and available analytics data remain to be confirmed.

### 3.6 Customer feedback and staff response

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FBK-01 | Customers shall be able to provide optional feedback after their stay or use of a service. |
| FBK-02 | Customers shall be able to submit feedback immediately during their stay or service experience. |
| FBK-03 | Customers shall be able to report service or equipment problems and indicate when they want corrective action, such as an equipment replacement. |
| FBK-04 | Customers shall be able to submit suggestions for future system features through the feedback facility. |
| FBK-05 | The system shall notify relevant staff of actionable customer feedback and make that feedback accessible to available staff for response. |
| FBK-06 | The system shall provide staff with suggested actions or guidance for handling customer issues. |
| FBK-07 | Feedback collection shall be optional and designed to avoid excessive or intrusive requests. |

The notification mechanism—such as email, an internal dashboard, or both—requires confirmation.

### 3.7 External service integration

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| INT-01 | The system shall support integration with social media platforms for promotional activities. |
| INT-02 | The system shall integrate with a weather and snow-condition data provider. |
| INT-03 | The system shall support integration with existing ski lift information systems where usable interfaces are available. |
| INT-04 | The system shall allow future integration with external equipment or service providers as business needs arise. |

## 4. Non-functional requirements

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| NFR-01 | The customer website shall be usable on mobile devices and desktop computers through a responsive interface. |
| NFR-02 | The system shall provide a modern, aesthetically pleasing customer interface. |
| NFR-03 | Customer, staff, and management interfaces shall be easy to learn and use. Usability acceptance criteria shall be agreed and evaluated before launch. |
| NFR-04 | The system shall protect customer and business information against unauthorized access, disclosure, and modification. |
| NFR-05 | Payment processing shall use appropriate security controls and comply with applicable payment-security obligations. |
| NFR-06 | The system shall include safeguards against attacks and service disruption. Specific availability, recovery, and security targets require definition. |
| NFR-07 | The system shall comply with applicable privacy, payment, accommodation, and other relevant legal or industry requirements. Applicable obligations shall be established during detailed requirements analysis. |
| NFR-08 | The system shall be extensible to accommodate additional resorts and services, including services unrelated to skiing. |
| NFR-09 | The system shall allow customer reviews, personalized recommendations, and loyalty functionality to be added without substantial disruption to existing functions. |

## 5. Implementation and acceptance requirements

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| DEP-01 | Implementation and transition to the new system shall not interrupt the resorts’ day-to-day operations. |
| DEP-02 | A transition approach shall be established for consolidating information currently held in the resorts’ local databases. Data scope, migration rules, and reconciliation procedures require agreement. |
| DEP-03 | The owner and selected employees shall be able to test the system before general release. |
| DEP-04 | Pre-release testing shall allow selected customers to participate if the owner elects to include them. |
| DEP-05 | Pre-release testing shall assess usability for customers, staff, and the owner. |

## 6. Future or uncommitted capabilities

These capabilities were discussed but are not confirmed initial-release commitments:

- Customer reviews and personalized recommendations: the stakeholder wants the ability to add them.
- Loyalty programs: potential future addition.
- Additional resorts and non-ski services, such as hiking trips.
- Virtual resort tours: nice to have; photographs are the initial preference.
- Customer messaging service: undecided.
- External equipment-provider integrations: desired as needed; no initial provider or workflow identified.

## 7. Key requirements requiring clarification

Before implementation, confirm:

1. **Booking rules:** Availability calculations, room occupancy, class capacity, rental periods, pricing, and prevention of conflicting reservations.
2. **Booking lifecycle:** Confirmation, amendments, cancellations, refunds, check-in/check-out, and equipment return processes.
3. **Payments:** Accepted methods, payment timing, deposits, taxes, and handling of failed payments.
4. **Staff permissions:** Access boundaries between resorts and authority for financial corrections or record removal.
5. **Reports:** Required contents, formats, schedules, and promotion-success measures.
6. **Integrations:** Target social platforms, partner systems, weather providers, and ski lift data availability.
7. **Feedback handling:** Notification recipients, response responsibilities, and issue-resolution workflow.
8. **Quality targets:** Expected transaction volumes, response times, availability, recovery objectives, and measurable usability criteria.
9. **Transition:** Records to migrate, treatment of existing reservations, and how legacy operations will continue during rollout.
10. **Release priorities:** Which desired features must be available at launch versus delivered later.
