# Software Requirements: Ski Resort Website and Management System

## 1. Purpose and Scope

The system will provide a public website and centralized internal management capabilities for a business operating three ski resorts.

Its business objectives are to:
- Attract new customers and increase the resorts’ visibility.
- Allow customers to book resort visits and training classes online.
- Streamline reservation management, recruitment, and internal administration across the three resorts.

## 2. Functional Requirements

### 2.1 Resort Information and Promotion

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-01 | The website shall present information about the three ski resorts and their offerings to prospective customers. |
| FR-02 | The website shall provide access to reservation, training-class booking, and job-listing functions. |

### 2.2 Reservations and Availability

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-03 | The system shall allow customers to make reservations for visits to the resorts. |
| FR-04 | The system shall allow staff to manage customer reservations. The required management actions remain to be defined. |
| FR-05 | The website shall display a customer-accessible calendar showing resort opening dates or times and real-time booking availability. |
| FR-06 | The reservation process shall use current availability when accepting bookings. |
| FR-07 | The system shall support booking-calendar synchronization. The calendars, synchronization direction, and synchronization frequency remain to be confirmed. |
| FR-08 | The system shall support customer profiles for use in the booking process. Required profile information and account workflows remain to be defined. |
| FR-09 | The system shall integrate with a payment gateway to support online booking payments. The provider and payment workflow remain to be selected. |

### 2.3 Training Classes

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-10 | The system shall allow customers to book ski training classes. |

Class schedules, capacity, pricing, instructor allocation, and their relationship to resort-visit reservations require further clarification.

### 2.4 Weather Information

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-11 | The website shall display weather forecasts for the ski resorts to help customers make booking decisions. |
| FR-12 | The system shall obtain updated weather information for each customer weather inquiry. What constitutes an inquiry remains to be defined. |
| FR-13 | The system shall integrate with a third-party weather service. No provider has been selected. |

The system must not assume that forecasts are available for every future booking date; the required behavior outside the provider’s forecast range needs confirmation.

### 2.5 Recruitment

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-14 | The website shall publish job listings for the resorts. |
| FR-15 | The system shall support staff management of job listings and job applications. |

Whether candidates must submit applications through the website, and the required application-processing workflow, remain to be confirmed.

### 2.6 Centralized Internal Administration

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-16 | The system shall provide a centralized database supporting internal administration across all three resorts. |
| FR-17 | The system shall support centralized management of accounting and tax-related information for the resorts. The precise functions and data requirements remain to be defined. |
| FR-18 | The system shall restrict internal administrative data and functions to authorized internal users. |
| FR-19 | The system shall expose opening-calendar and booking-availability information to customers without exposing other internal administrative data. |

Existing accounting and management software must be identified with the stakeholder’s assistant before integration requirements can be specified.

## 3. Quality Requirements

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| QR-01 | The public website shall be aesthetically pleasing. Branding, design direction, and approval criteria require agreement with the stakeholder. |
| QR-02 | The website shall be easy to use, particularly for viewing availability and completing bookings. Usability acceptance criteria remain to be established. |
| QR-03 | Customer-facing availability shall be maintained in real time. The maximum acceptable update delay and handling of simultaneous bookings require definition. |
| QR-04 | The system design shall allow special-event functionality to be added in the future. |

Security requirements beyond the internal-access restriction, as well as performance, availability, accessibility, backup, and privacy requirements, were not established in the interview and require elicitation.

## 4. Delivery Constraint

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| DC-01 | The website is targeted for completion approximately one year after the interview. The exact delivery date, milestones, and release scope require confirmation. |

## 5. Future Scope

Special events are not currently planned for the initial release. The design must accommodate their future addition, but event functionality and workflows have not been specified.

## 6. Key Outstanding Decisions

Before implementation and acceptance planning, confirm:
- Reservation types, capacity rules, pricing, cancellations, refunds, and staff management actions.
- Customer registration, profile data, and authentication workflows.
- Payment provider, currencies, and payment-processing rules.
- Calendar synchronization targets and timing.
- Training-class scheduling and booking rules.
- Weather provider, refresh triggers, and unavailable-forecast behavior.
- Recruitment submission and application-management workflows.
- Accounting and tax scope, existing software, and integration needs.
- Internal user roles and access permissions.
- Measurable quality criteria and the exact launch date.
