# Software Requirements: Cool Ski Resorts

## 1. Purpose and scope

The system will digitize and centralize booking-related operations across the hotel entities of **Cool Ski Resorts**. It will support shared processes for reservations, customer service, and payments, alongside social media automation and website content integration.

### Stakeholders
- **Customers:** View availability and prices and use customer-facing processes.
- **Employees:** Operate shared hotel processes and manage content.
- **Owners:** Oversee operations, costs, promotions, and customer insights.
- **Developers:** Integrate, maintain, and adapt the system.
- **External providers:** Supply payment, social media, content management, and weather services.

Specific permissions for these stakeholders remain to be defined.

## 2. Business priorities

The stakeholder specified the following order:

1. Identify processes that can or should be shared across hotel entities.
2. Implement those processes digitally.
3. Automate social media publishing.
4. Integrate social media content into the website.

Identifying and agreeing on shared processes is a prerequisite to detailed implementation, rather than necessarily a software feature.

## 3. Functional requirements

### 3.1 Centralized operations

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-01 | The system shall provide a centralized database shared across the participating hotel entities for booking-related operations. |
| FR-02 | The system shall support standardized reservation processes across hotel entities. |
| FR-03 | The system shall support standardized customer-service processes across hotel entities. |
| FR-04 | The system shall support standardized payment processes across hotel entities. |

The detailed workflows and data required for FR-02 through FR-04 must be established during shared-process analysis.

### 3.2 Availability, pricing, and promotions

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-05 | The system shall allow users to view which rooms are available for specified dates and their corresponding prices. |
| FR-06 | The system shall allow staff to highlight selected dates or periods for promotion, including periods of low demand. |
| FR-07 | The system shall allow staff to configure discounts for selected dates or periods, including periods of low demand. |
| FR-08 | The system shall capture and present information about what prospective customers search for, to support understanding of customer demand. |

The audience and presentation for highlighted dates, discount rules, and the scope of search analytics require clarification.

### 3.3 Social media automation

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-09 | The solution shall support managing social media publishing across multiple platforms through an integrated tool. |
| FR-10 | The social media tool shall allow users to prepare and schedule posts in advance. |
| FR-11 | The social media tool shall automatically publish scheduled posts to the selected supported platforms at the scheduled times. |
| FR-12 | The solution shall support collecting or accessing insights about current and prospective customers through its social media tooling. |

The stakeholder prefers coverage of as many social media platforms as practical. The required platforms and customer-insight capabilities have not yet been specified.

### 3.4 Shared content and website integration

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-13 | The solution shall support entering and managing post content in a shared content source for use by both social media automation and the website. |
| FR-14 | The website shall present content from that shared source in a blog-like format. |
| FR-15 | Website posts shall support displaying images alongside post text. |
| FR-16 | The website shall display current weather information obtained through a weather API. |

A headless CMS, such as Contentful, was suggested as an implementation approach; no particular product was mandated.

## 4. Quality requirements and constraints

| ID | Requirement or constraint |
|---|---|
| NFR-01 | Employee-facing and customer-facing functions shall be designed for simplicity, minimizing unnecessary steps and complications. |
| NFR-02 | Employee-facing functions shall minimize the effort and cost required to train new employees. |
| NFR-03 | The solution shall be adaptable to the organization's agreed processes and future process changes. |
| NFR-04 | The solution architecture shall favor a small, low-complexity implementation to reduce the time and cost of maintenance, adjustments, and new features. |
| CON-01 | Selection shall favor integration and adaptation of an existing system over development entirely from scratch. |
| CON-02 | The selected core system should be open source or available with purchasable source code so that it can be modified. |
| CON-03 | An existing payment provider, such as Stripe, shall be evaluated before deciding to implement payment processing from scratch. Stripe is an example, not a mandated provider. |
| CON-04 | Payment-provider selection shall include a comparison with a self-implemented alternative over a 10-year operating period. The stakeholder requires provider fees not to exceed the comparable self-implemented cost. The comparison basis must be agreed; discounted cash flow is a suggested evaluation method. |

Measurable usability, training, maintainability, and cost thresholds were not established in the interview.

## 5. Business objectives and success criteria

The solution is intended to:

- Make shared reservation, customer-service, and payment processes operate effectively across hotel entities.
- Reduce operational complications, training costs, and change costs.
- Attract new customer segments and increase visibility.
- Improve understanding of existing and prospective customers.
- Strengthen the brand and relationships with existing customers.
- Keep the website updated using content also prepared for social media.

The stakeholder defines success as **working processes and satisfied stakeholders**. These criteria require measurable acceptance targets before deployment approval.

## 6. Outstanding requirements to clarify

1. **Shared workflows:** Detailed reservation, customer-service, and payment steps, including permitted differences between hotels.
2. **Booking scope:** Whether customers book directly online and what booking changes, cancellations, or related operations are required.
3. **Centralized data:** Required records, existing data sources, migration needs, and visibility between hotel entities.
4. **Access control:** User roles and permissions.
5. **Payments:** Required methods, currencies, refunds, and assumptions for the 10-year cost comparison.
6. **Promotions:** Discount rules and where highlighted dates appear.
7. **Analytics:** Search information to capture, required reports, and the intended social media insights.
8. **Social media:** Required platforms, account structure, scheduling behavior, and handling of publishing failures.
9. **Website content:** Content workflow and timing of website publication relative to social media publication.
10. **Weather:** Relevant resort locations, weather provider, and refresh frequency.
11. **Quality and compliance:** Security, privacy, accessibility, availability, performance, backup, and recovery requirements.
12. **Acceptance:** Quantitative criteria for usability, training effort, cost, process effectiveness, and stakeholder satisfaction.
