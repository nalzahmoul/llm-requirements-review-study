# Software Requirements: Online Resort Management System

## 1. Purpose and scope

The system shall support the digital management of three ski resorts through an online customer website and resort management capabilities. It shall support accommodation reservations, rental hiring, training-class bookings, payments, inventory management, and customer-facing promotional and information services.

The business objectives are to:
- Reduce manual administration and staffing needs.
- Improve transparency in the booking process.
- Reduce coordination effort across the three resorts.
- Allow customers to discover available offerings and book online easily.

These are business objectives; measurable success targets have not yet been defined.

## 2. Functional requirements

### 2.1 Website and resort information

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| WEB-01 | The system shall provide a customer-facing website covering all three resorts. |
| WEB-02 | The website shall allow customers to view the offerings and availability at each resort. |
| WEB-03 | The website shall provide clearly labeled navigation controls that distinguish viewing information from making a booking. |

### 2.2 Reservations and bookings

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| BKG-01 | The system shall allow customers to make accommodation reservations online. |
| BKG-02 | The system shall allow customers to book rental items online. |
| BKG-03 | The system shall allow customers to book training classes online. |
| BKG-04 | The system shall guide customers through a step-by-step booking process. |
| BKG-05 | The booking process shall allow customers to select optional add-ons and customize their booking package. |
| BKG-06 | Supported booking options shall include flights, breakfast, accommodation-only stays without a ski pass, bus tickets, restaurant vouchers, and insurance. |
| BKG-07 | The system shall support staff management of reservations and bookings across the three resorts. |

The rules, availability, pricing, and fulfillment arrangements for booking options remain to be defined.

### 2.3 Payments

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| PAY-01 | The system shall support online payment for reservations and bookings. |
| PAY-02 | The system shall support multiple payment methods to accommodate a broad range of customers. |

Specific payment methods and payment providers have not been selected. The stakeholder requested as many payment options as possible, but this requires an agreed, feasible list.

### 2.4 Inventory management

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| INV-01 | The system shall support management of rental inventory for the resorts. |
| INV-02 | The system shall support management of on-site operational inventory, including the household or utility items referred to by the stakeholder. |

The inventory categories and required tracking operations have not yet been specified.

### 2.5 Social media promotion

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| SOC-01 | The system shall support resort promotion through Twitter, Facebook, and Instagram. |

The required integration behavior—such as profile links, content sharing, embedded feeds, or publishing tools—remains to be determined.

### 2.6 Weather and snow information

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| WTH-01 | The system shall allow customers to view weather and snow-condition forecasts relevant to their resort. |
| WTH-02 | The system shall obtain forecast information from suitable local weather and snow-information services. |

The service providers and criteria for selecting the “best” local services remain to be agreed.

### 2.7 Customer feedback

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| REV-01 | The website shall support customer feedback through Google Reviews. |

Whether this requires links for submitting reviews, displaying existing reviews, or both remains to be confirmed.

## 3. User interface and quality requirements

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| UX-01 | The website shall use a ski-themed visual design. |
| UX-02 | The website shall be aesthetically pleasing and intuitive for customers. |
| UX-03 | The booking interface shall allow customers to complete bookings easily and promptly. |
| UX-04 | The interface shall make the paths to resort information and booking clearly distinguishable. |

Usability acceptance criteria and booking response-time targets must be defined before these qualitative requirements can be tested objectively. The stakeholder’s request for “instant” booking does not yet establish a precise performance target or confirmation rule.

## 4. Stakeholder-approved priorities

The stakeholder approved the following order:

1. Website development
2. Online reservations and bookings
3. Payment processing
4. Social media promotion
5. Weather and snow forecasts
6. Inventory management
7. Customer feedback
8. Logo design

## 5. Related non-software deliverable

A new resort logo is a potential branding deliverable. The stakeholder is open to new design concepts and has not specified existing elements that must be retained. Logo creation should be treated separately from software functionality.

## 6. Outstanding requirements and decisions

The following matters were not resolved in the interview and should not be treated as agreed requirements:

- **Multi-resort administration:** Staff roles, permissions, shared data, and resort-specific access.
- **Booking rules:** Availability calculation, pricing, capacity limits, booking confirmation, amendments, cancellations, and prevention of conflicting bookings.
- **Add-on fulfillment:** Whether flights, transport, insurance, and other options require external provider integrations or manual handling.
- **Payments:** Supported methods, currencies, deposits, refunds, receipts, and payment-failure handling.
- **Inventory:** Item categories, quantities, locations, rental issue/return tracking, and maintenance needs.
- **Existing data:** Whether local resort databases must be migrated, replaced, or integrated.
- **Financial administration:** Whether accounting, taxation, financial reporting, or accounting-system integration is in scope. These were described as existing activities, not explicitly requested features.
- **External integrations:** Exact Google Reviews, social media, and forecast integration behavior.
- **Quality and operational requirements:** Security, privacy, accessibility, availability, supported devices, backup, recovery, and measurable performance targets.
- **Branding:** Whether the new logo is a committed deliverable and whether branding covers each resort individually or all three collectively.
