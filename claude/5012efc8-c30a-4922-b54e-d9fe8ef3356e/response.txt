# Software Requirements Specification: Cool Ski Resorts Online System

**Stakeholder:** Jim, Owner, Cool Ski Resorts
**Source:** Requirements elicitation interview (ended early at the stakeholder's request)

**Legend for "Basis" column:**
- **Stated**: the stakeholder said it explicitly.
- **Accepted**: the interviewer suggested it and the stakeholder agreed.
- **Inferred**: the reviewer derived it from context. It needs confirmation.

---

## 1. Project Overview

Cool Ski Resorts runs three resorts. It needs an online system that lets customers:
- reserve hotel rooms,
- hire ski equipment,
- book training classes, and
- view weather and snow conditions,

all through a user-friendly website. The system will use one centralized database to manage reservations, bookings, and inventory across all three resorts. It will also connect to social media for promotion.

**Business goals:**
- Streamline operations and reduce staff workload.
- Improve coordination between the three resorts.
- Increase visibility and reach a larger customer base.
- Let customers book from home without phoning or waiting in a queue.

## 2. Current State

| Area | Current Process | Problem |
|---|---|---|
| Reservations, hire, classes | Semi-manual, by telephone and fax | Slow, labor-intensive, and customers must call and wait |
| Data storage | A separate local database at each resort | No shared view and little coordination |
| Payments | Processed manually on-site by staff | No online payment capability |
| Inventory | Tracked manually per resort. Staff update records when items are rented, returned, or maintained | No central overview, prone to errors, and no coordination between resorts |

## 3. Users

| User | Description |
|---|---|
| Customer / Guest | Browses resort offerings, makes bookings, pays online, and checks conditions |
| Resort Staff | Manage inventory, accounts, and customer service at each resort |
| Management / Owner | Monitors inventory and historical data across all resorts *(Inferred)* |

## 6. Constraints and Assumptions

- **C-1:** The system must serve three resorts from one centralized database. This replaces the current separate local databases.
- **C-2:** The stakeholder wants the booking system as soon as possible ("Tomorrow"). This is not a realistic timeline, but it shows that booking and payment are urgent. Delivering these first, in a phased approach, is recommended.
- **A-1:** Data from the existing local databases at each resort will need to be migrated. *(Inferred. Not discussed.)*
- **A-2:** Staff will keep handling physical rentals and returns, recording them in the new system. *(Inferred.)*

## 7. Open Issues for Follow-Up

The interview ended early, so these points need answers:

1. **Staff devices:** Should staff use the inventory system and dashboard on desktop, mobile, or both? This was asked but not answered.
2. **Notifications:** Which notifications are needed (confirmations, reminders, cancellations), and through which channels? This was asked but not answered.
3. **Priorities:** Only booking and payment were ranked. The other features still need to be ranked.
4. **Weather access:** Are conditions public, or only for "customers staying at the resort"? The stakeholder's two statements differ.
5. **Social media scope:** Which platforms are involved? Is it automated posting, share buttons, paid ads, or something else?
6. **Customer support:** Should it be live chat with staff, a chatbot, or email/ticketing? What hours should it cover?
7. **Booking rules:** What are the cancellation and refund policies, deposits, and package or bundled bookings (e.g., hotel plus equipment plus class)?
8. **Customer accounts:** Should customers register accounts, or can they book as guests?
9. **On-site payment:** Should on-site payment remain an option alongside online payment?
10. **Dashboard content:** Which metrics, reports, and time ranges are needed (e.g., utilization, maintenance history)?
11. **Languages and currencies:** Are multiple languages or currencies needed for international guests?
12. **Budget and timeline:** What are the realistic budget and delivery milestones?

## 8. Recommended Release Phasing

1. **Phase 1 (urgent):**
   - Website with resort offerings.
   - Online hotel, equipment-hire, and class booking.
   - Online payments (credit card, debit card, PayPal).
   - The centralized database.
2. **Phase 2:**
   - Centralized inventory management with real-time tracking and the visual dashboard.
3. **Phase 3:**
   - Weather and snow conditions.
   - Social media integration.
   - Online customer support.
