# Software Requirements Specification
**Project:** Cool Ski Resorts – Online Reservation, Booking & Payment System
**Stakeholder:** Jim Smith (Co-owner, Cool Ski Resorts)
**Source:** Requirements elicitation interview (incomplete. The stakeholder left before the detailed elicitation finished.)

---

## 1. Background and Context

Cool Ski Resorts is a small, family-run chain of **three U.S.-based ski resorts**. Today:

- Customers make reservations by **phone or fax**, contacting each resort directly.
- Each resort records room bookings, ski equipment rentals and training class registrations in its **own local database**.
- Guests **pay on arrival or departure**. Staff enter payment details manually.
- Each resort has its **own team of assistants** who manage reservations, payments, taxation and financial records.
- Coordinating inventory and customer data between resorts takes **significant manual effort**. It is error-prone and limits scalability.

**Business goals:**
- BG-1: Improve operational efficiency and reduce manual errors.
- BG-2: Improve customer experience through self-service, 24/7 booking.
- BG-3: Increase customer reach and online visibility.
- BG-4: Allow the business to scale.
- BG-5: Achieve the above with **minimal disruption to current operations**.

---

## 2. Stakeholders and User Classes

| User Class | Description |
|---|---|
| Customer / Guest | Books rooms, equipment and classes online, pays online, receives a confirmation, and views weather and snow conditions. |
| Resort Staff (Assistants) | Manage reservations, check guests in by scanning QR codes or booking numbers, and track payments and inventory. |
| Owners / Management | Oversee operations and financials across all three resorts. |
| Prospective Customers | Find the resorts through the website and social media. |

---

## 3. Scope and Priorities

The stakeholder set this implementation order for the six core features:

| Priority | Feature |
|---|---|
| 1 | Online Reservations and Bookings |
| 2 | Centralized Database |
| 3 | Integrated Payment System |
| 4 | Inventory Management |
| 5 | Social Media Integration |
| 6 | Real-Time Weather and Snow Conditions |

Three features are **future** scope: Customer Reviews, Loyalty Program and Mobile Optimization. They are not in the current release, but the system must be designed so they can be added later without major rework (see Section 6).

## 4. Functional Requirements

### 4.1 Online Reservations and Bookings (Priority 1)

| ID | Requirement | Source |
|---|---|---|
| FR-1.1 | The system shall allow customers to book **hotel rooms** online at any of the three resorts. | Stated |
| FR-1.2 | The system shall allow customers to book **ski equipment rentals** online. | Stated |
| FR-1.3 | The system shall allow customers to register for **training classes** online. | Stated |
| FR-1.4 | The booking service shall be available to customers **24/7** without staff involvement. | Stated |
| FR-1.5 | On a completed booking, the system shall send the customer a **confirmation email**. | Stated |
| FR-1.6 | Each confirmation email shall contain a **unique booking number and/or a QR code** identifying the booking. | Stated |
| FR-1.7 | Resort staff shall be able to **scan the QR code** (or enter the booking number) with a phone to retrieve the booking and check the guest in, without the customer repeating an entry or registration process. | Stated (interviewer's summary; not explicitly confirmed) |
| FR-1.8 | The system shall only accept bookings for rooms, equipment and class places that are actually available, using inventory data (see 4.4). | Derived |

### 4.2 Centralized Database (Priority 2)

| ID | Requirement | Source |
|---|---|---|
| FR-2.1 | The system shall provide a **single, unified database** shared by all three resorts, replacing the separate local databases. | Stated |
| FR-2.2 | The database shall store **reservations, customer data, inventory and financial data** for all resorts. | Stated |
| FR-2.3 | Data changes shall be visible to all resorts **in real time**. | Stated |
| FR-2.4 | Authorized staff and management shall be able to view and manage data across resorts for coordination. | Derived |
| FR-2.5 | The system shall support the financial and **taxation record-keeping** that each resort currently handles locally. | Derived from current process |
| FR-2.6 | Existing data from the local resort databases shall be **migrated** into the central database. | Derived (minimal disruption) |

### 4.3 Integrated Payment System (Priority 3)

| ID | Requirement | Source |
|---|---|---|
| FR-3.1 | The system shall allow customers to **pay online** for bookings and services. | Stated |
| FR-3.2 | The system shall support both **deposit payments** and **full payments**. | Stated |
| FR-3.3 | Payments shall be **recorded automatically** in the central database against the booking, so staff do not enter them manually. | Stated |
| FR-3.4 | The payment process shall be **simple** and not overcomplicated for customers. | Stated |
| FR-3.5 | The system shall record the outstanding balance on any booking paid by deposit. | Derived |

### 4.4 Inventory Management (Priority 4)

| ID | Requirement | Source |
|---|---|---|
| FR-4.1 | The system shall track **ski equipment availability** in real time. | Stated |
| FR-4.2 | The system shall track **room occupancy** in real time. | Stated |
| FR-4.3 | The system shall track **other resort resources**, such as training class capacity, in real time. | Stated / Derived |
| FR-4.4 | The system shall **prevent overbooking** and resource shortages. | Stated |
| FR-4.5 | Inventory shall update automatically when bookings are created, changed or cancelled. | Derived |

### 4.5 Social Media Integration (Priority 5)

| ID | Requirement | Source |
|---|---|---|
| FR-5.1 | The website shall link to the company's **Facebook, Instagram and Twitter (X)** accounts. | Stated |
| FR-5.2 | The social media integration shall support **promotion** of the resorts and **direct interaction** with potential guests. | Stated |

### 4.6 Weather and Snow Conditions (Priority 6)

| ID | Requirement | Source |
|---|---|---|
| FR-6.1 | The website shall display **real-time weather conditions and forecasts** for each resort. | Stated |
| FR-6.2 | The website shall display **real-time snow conditions** for each resort. | Stated |

## 5. Non-Functional Requirements

| ID | Category | Requirement | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| NFR-1 | Usability | The booking and payment process shall be simple, with **very high usability** for non-technical customers. | Stated |
| NFR-2 | Availability | The customer-facing booking system shall be available **24/7**. | Stated |
| NFR-3 | Security | Online payments shall be processed **securely**. Because the system operates in the U.S. and handles card payments, it is expected to comply with **PCI DSS**. | Stated / Derived |
| NFR-4 | Security / Privacy | Customer personal data shall be protected against unauthorized access. Staff access shall be restricted by role. | Derived |
| NFR-5 | Data Consistency | Reservation, inventory and payment data shall be consistent and up to date across all resorts in real time. | Stated |
| NFR-6 | Reliability | The system shall reduce manual errors compared with the current process. | Stated (goal) |
| NFR-7 | Scalability | The system shall support business growth, such as more customers, services or resorts. | Stated (goal) |
| NFR-8 | Extensibility | The architecture shall allow the future features in Section 6 to be added without major redesign. | Stated |
| NFR-9 | Transition | Rollout shall cause **minimal disruption** to current resort operations. | Stated |
| NFR-10 | Portability | QR code scanning for check-in shall work on standard staff **mobile phones**. | Stated |

## 6. Future Features (Design for Extensibility)

These are not in the initial release. The stakeholder asked that they be planned for now "to spare time and energy" later.

| ID | Feature | Description |
|---|---|---|
| FF-1 | Customer Reviews and Testimonials | Guests can leave feedback and reviews to build trust and credibility. |
| FF-2 | Loyalty Program | Repeat customers can sign up for rewards or discounts. |
| FF-3 | Mobile Optimization | The website is fully responsive and easy to use on mobile devices. |

> **Reviewer note:** Mobile optimization is listed as future scope, but the stakeholder said "many customers may prefer booking from their phones," and NFR-1 requires very high usability. I recommend confirming whether basic responsive design should be part of the first release.

---

## 7. Constraints and Assumptions

**Constraints**
- C-1: The system must serve **three resorts located in the U.S.** U.S. tax and payment regulations apply.
- C-2: The client is a **small business**, so budget and IT resources are presumably limited (to be confirmed).

**Assumptions**
- A-1: Currency is USD.
- A-2: Customers will provide an email address when booking, needed for FR-1.5.
- A-3: Weather and snow data will come from an external third-party service.
- A-4: A third-party payment provider will be used rather than in-house card processing.

## 8. Open Issues and Items Requiring Clarification

The interview ended during the detailed discussion of the first feature. These points need a follow-up session:

**General**
1. The recap of the booking feature (FR-1.5 to FR-1.7) was **not confirmed** by the stakeholder.
2. When asked about bookings, the stakeholder spoke of "facilitating the **payment** process." Confirm whether this meant booking, payment, or both.
3. Detailed requirements for features 2–6 were **not discussed** and need elicitation.
4. Budget, timeline and success criteria are unknown.

**Reservations**
5. Can customers modify or cancel bookings online? What are the cancellation and refund policies?
6. Are customer accounts and logins required, or is guest checkout acceptable?
7. Should phone, fax and walk-in bookings continue? If so, how will staff enter them into the new system?
8. Can customers combine rooms, equipment and classes in a single booking?
9. How should pricing work, including seasonal rates and packages?

**Payments**
10. Which payment methods should be supported, such as credit cards or PayPal?
11. Which deposit rules apply, such as the amount and when the balance is due?
12. Should on-site payment remain an option?
13. How should taxes be calculated and reported per resort?

**Database and Operations**
14. Which user roles and permissions are needed, for example staff at one resort vs. management?
15. What reporting is needed, for example financial, occupancy or inventory reports?
16. What data must be migrated, and in what condition is the current data?
17. Does QR check-in need to work offline in areas with poor connectivity?

**Other**
18. Is a preferred weather or snow data provider required? What does "real-time" mean in terms of update frequency?
19. Does social media integration mean only links, or also embedded feeds and sharing?
20. Are other languages required besides English?

---

## 9. Summary

The client needs a centralized, web-based system that lets customers book rooms, equipment rentals and training classes and pay for them online at any of three resorts, with email confirmations and QR-code check-in. A shared real-time database will replace the separate local systems for reservations, inventory and financial data. Social media links and weather and snow information will boost visibility and customer experience. Reviews, loyalty and mobile optimization are planned for later. **A follow-up interview is needed** to confirm the booking requirements and elicit details for the remaining features.
