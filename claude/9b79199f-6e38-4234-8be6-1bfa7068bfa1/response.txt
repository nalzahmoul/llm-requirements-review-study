# Software Requirements Specification: Cool Ski Resorts Online Reservation & Management System

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Purpose
This document states the software requirements for Cool Ski Resorts, based on the elicitation interview with the owner (Mary/Jim Smith). It also records reviewer interpretations, conflicts, and open issues that must be resolved before design.

### 1.2 Business Context
- Cool Ski Resorts runs **three new ski resorts** in different US mountain locations.
- Services offered:
  - Hotel room bookings
  - Ski equipment and clothing hire
  - Ski training classes
- **Current state:**
  - The website has static content only (services and contact information).
  - Reservations and hires are taken by telephone and fax.
  - Payments are handled semi-manually at each resort.
  - Each resort stores its data in its own local database.
  - Coordination across the three resorts is becoming difficult.
  - The resorts have very few customers ("I don't have customers").
- **Business goals:**
  - **G1:** Increase visibility to customers across the US and grow revenue.
  - **G2:** Streamline operations across the three resorts through centralized data.
  - **G3:** Make report generation easier.
  - **G4:** Stand out from competitors through innovative digital features.
  - **G5:** Introduce the new system without significant disruption to current operations.

### 1.3 Stakeholders and Users

| Role | Description |
|---|---|
| Owner (Mary/Jim Smith) | Business owner and project sponsor; consumer of cross-resort reports |
| Customers | Prospective and current guests across the US |
| Customer service staff | Handle reservations and customer enquiries at each resort |
| Inventory staff | Manage ski equipment and clothing stock at each resort |
| Accounts / taxation assistants | Manage accounts, taxation, and booking finances at each resort |
| Marketing / content staff (internal) | Create the weekly social media content |

### 1.4 Source Notation
Each requirement is tagged by how firmly it is supported by the interview:
- **[S] Stated:** the stakeholder said it directly.
- **[C] Confirmed:** the interviewer proposed it and the stakeholder agreed, often with a one-word "Yes". These need validation.
- **[D] Derived:** the reviewer inferred it as necessary to meet a stated goal.

### 1.5 Reviewer Interpretations
- "Post about **jews** from the resort" is interpreted as a typo for "**news**".
- "Facebooka" is interpreted as Facebook.
- "US weather forecast system" is interpreted as the **National Weather Service (NWS / NOAA)** public API. This needs confirmation.

## 2. Functional Requirements

### 2.1 Public Website

| ID | Requirement | Source |
|---|---|---|
| FR-1 | The system shall provide a public website presenting the services available at each of the three resorts: accommodation, equipment/clothing hire, and training classes. | [S] |
| FR-2 | The website shall let customers view available services per resort, including descriptions, pictures, and prices. | [S]/[D] |
| FR-3 | The website shall display contact information for each resort, preserving the content of the existing static site. | [D] |
| FR-4 | Authorized staff shall be able to update website content (text, images, prices, news) without developer involvement. | [D] |

### 2.2 Hotel Reservations

| ID | Requirement | Source |
|---|---|---|
| FR-5 | Customers shall be able to search hotel room availability by resort, date range, and number of guests. | [S]/[D] |
| FR-6 | Customers shall be able to reserve one or more available rooms online. | [S] |
| FR-7 | The system shall prevent double booking of the same room for overlapping dates, across both online and staff-entered reservations. | [D] |
| FR-8 | Customers shall receive a booking confirmation, for example by email, containing a unique booking reference. | [D] |
| FR-9 | Customers shall be able to view, modify, or cancel their reservations, subject to a cancellation policy (TBD). | [D] |

### 2.3 Equipment and Clothing Hire

| ID | Requirement | Source |
|---|---|---|
| FR-10 | Customers shall be able to browse ski equipment and clothing available for hire at each resort. | [S] |
| FR-11 | Customers shall be able to reserve hire items for specified dates, selecting sizes where applicable. | [S]/[D] |
| FR-12 | Hire reservations shall check against and update the inventory of the relevant resort. | [S]/[D] |

### 2.4 Training Class Booking

| ID | Requirement | Source |
|---|---|---|
| FR-13 | Customers shall be able to view scheduled ski training classes per resort, including date, time, level, and remaining capacity. | [S]/[D] |
| FR-14 | Customers shall be able to book places in training classes. The system shall not allow a class to exceed its capacity. | [S]/[D] |
| FR-15 | Staff shall be able to create, edit, and cancel training class schedules. | [D] |

### 2.5 Online Payment

| ID | Requirement | Source |
|---|---|---|
| FR-16 | The system shall allow customers to pay online for reservations, hires, and class bookings. | [S] |
| FR-17 | The system shall integrate with a third-party payment provider (TBD). It shall not store full card details itself. | [D] |
| FR-18 | The system shall record all payment transactions in the central database, including amount, resort, service, date, and status. | [S]/[D] |
| FR-19 | The system shall support refunds for eligible cancellations. | [D] |
| FR-20 | The system shall continue to support recording payments taken at the resort for walk-in, telephone, or fax bookings. | [D] (G5) |

### 2.6 Weather and Snow Conditions

| ID | Requirement | Source |
|---|---|---|
| FR-21 | The system shall retrieve weather forecasts for each resort's region from the US national weather service (assumed NWS/NOAA API). | [S] |
| FR-22 | The system shall display snow conditions for each resort. | [S] |
| FR-23 | Weather and snow information shall be displayed on the **main page** and during the **booking process**. | [S]/[C] |
| FR-24 | Weather data shall be refreshed in near real time, at an interval no longer than the provider's update cycle (target TBD, e.g. ≤ 60 minutes). | [S] |
| FR-25 | If the weather service is unavailable, the system shall show the last retrieved data with its timestamp, or a clear "unavailable" message. Bookings shall not be blocked. | [D] |

> **Reviewer note:** NWS provides forecasts and some snowfall data, but on-mountain conditions such as snow depth, base, and runs open are typically not available from it. A supplementary source or manual staff entry may be required. See OI-4.

### 2.7 Social Media Integration

| ID | Requirement | Source |
|---|---|---|
| FR-26 | The website shall link to the company's Twitter (X), Facebook, and Instagram accounts. | [S] |
| FR-27 | The system shall support sharing promotions to these platforms. | [C] |
| FR-28 | The system shall support displaying user-generated content, such as resort photos, from these platforms. | [C] |
| FR-29 | The system shall support customer reviews and testimonials, displayed on the website and shareable to social media. | [C] |
| FR-30 | The business shall be able to publish a post at least **weekly** containing resort news, weather, and pictures. Content is created internally. | [S] |
| FR-31 | The system is **not** required to provide content planning or team-collaboration tools for social media. | [S] |

### 2.8 Centralized Database and Synchronization

| ID | Requirement | Source |
|---|---|---|
| FR-32 | The system shall maintain a centralized database of reservations, bookings, hires, payments, and inventory for all three resorts. | [S] |
| FR-33 | Each record shall be associated with the resort it belongs to. | [D] |
| FR-34 | Data shall be synchronized between the resort systems and the central database on a **scheduled** basis, not continuously. | [S] |
| FR-35 | Existing data from each resort's local database shall be migrated to the central database. | [D] |

> **Reviewer note:** FR-34 conflicts with FR-7, FR-12, and FR-14. See OI-1.

| ID | Requirement | Source |
|---|---|---|
| FR-44 | Staff shall authenticate to access back-office functions. | [D] |

## 3. Non-Functional Requirements

| ID | Category | Requirement | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| NFR-1 | Usability / Aesthetics | The website shall be aesthetically pleasing and intuitive. Measurable acceptance criteria are TBD, e.g. a new user completes a booking unaided in ≤ 5 minutes. | [S] |
| NFR-2 | Responsiveness | The website shall be usable on desktop and mobile devices. | [D] |
| NFR-3 | Accessibility | The website should conform to WCAG 2.1 AA. | [D] |
| NFR-4 | Security | Payment processing shall comply with PCI DSS, typically by delegating card handling to the payment provider. | [D] |
| NFR-5 | Security / Privacy | Customer personal data shall be protected in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. Access shall be restricted by role. | [D] |
| NFR-6 | Availability | The public booking site should be available 24/7, with a target uptime that is TBD. | [D] |
| NFR-7 | Scalability | The system shall accommodate growth from the current small customer base to nationwide demand. | [D] (G1) |
| NFR-8 | Data integrity | The system shall guarantee no double bookings and no overselling of inventory or class places. | [D] |
| NFR-9 | Transition | The system shall be introduced with minimal disruption to current operations. Phased rollout or parallel running is recommended. | [S] |
| NFR-10 | Maintainability | Integrations with the weather service, social media, and payment provider shall be isolated so that a provider can be changed without major rework. | [D] |

## 5. Conflicts and Open Issues

| ID | Issue | Recommendation / Question for Stakeholder |
|---|---|---|
| **OI-1** | **Sync conflict.** The stakeholder wants "scheduled, not frequent" synchronization. However, online booking across three resorts requires up-to-date availability. Infrequent sync risks double bookings between online and phone/fax bookings. | Recommend that the central database be the single source of truth for availability, checked in real time by both the website and staff. Scheduled sync could apply only to back-office or legacy data during transition. Confirm the stakeholder's intent and an acceptable frequency. |
| OI-2 | **Weak confirmations.** FR-27 to FR-29 and FR-39 to FR-42 were confirmed with a single "Yes" to multi-part questions. | Validate each feature and its priority individually. |
| OI-3 | **Who publishes weekly posts?** Content is created internally, but it is unclear whether posting happens manually on each platform or through the system. | Clarify whether the system must publish to social media or only link and embed. |
| OI-4 | **Snow conditions source.** The NWS may not provide resort-level snow conditions. | Decide between a supplementary data provider and manual staff entry. |
| OI-5 | **Payment details.** The payment provider, accepted methods, deposit vs. full payment, refund and cancellation policy, and tax handling are not specified. | Elicit these from the owner and the accounts assistants. |
| OI-6 | **Customer accounts.** It is unknown whether customers need registered accounts or can book as guests. It is also unknown whether combined packages (room + hire + class) are needed. | Clarify. |
| OI-7 | **Existing staff not consulted.** Problems with the current system were not elicited in detail ("It sucks"). | Interview customer service, inventory, and accounts staff at each resort. |
| OI-8 | **Reporting details.** Report frequency, format (on-screen, PDF, export), and recipients are not defined. | Clarify. |
| OI-9 | **Legacy channels.** It is unclear whether telephone and fax bookings will continue after launch. | Assumed yes (FR-20, FR-46). Confirm. |
| OI-10 | **Reviews moderation.** It is not defined whether customer reviews require approval before publication. | Clarify. |
| OI-11 | **Measurable quality criteria.** "Aesthetically pleasing", "intuitive", and "real-time" lack measurable definitions. | Agree on acceptance criteria. |
| OI-12 | **"Innovative solutions".** This goal is stated but not defined. | Explore options such as loyalty programs, dynamic pricing, mobile app, or package deals. |

## 6. Suggested Priorities (MoSCoW, pending stakeholder validation)

- **Must:**
  - Public website (FR-1 to FR-4)
  - Hotel, hire, and class booking (FR-5 to FR-15)
  - Online payment (FR-16 to FR-20)
  - Central SQL database (FR-32 to FR-35)
  - Inventory management (FR-36 to FR-38)
  - Staff access (FR-44 to FR-46)
  - Security and data integrity (NFR-4, NFR-5, NFR-8)
- **Should:**
  - Weather and snow conditions (FR-21 to FR-25)
  - Reporting (FR-39 to FR-43)
  - Social media links and weekly posts (FR-26, FR-30)
- **Could:**
  - Auto-sharing of promotions (FR-27)
  - User-generated content (FR-28)
  - Reviews and testimonials (FR-29)
- **Won't (this release):**
  - Internal content planning and collaboration tools (FR-31)
