# Software Requirements Specification: Cool Ski Resorts Online Booking & Management System

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Background
Cool Ski Resorts operates three ski resorts in different mountain regions of the US. Customers can book rooms, rent ski equipment and clothing, and book ski training classes.

The current setup has the following limitations:
- There is a single informational website that shows services and contact details, but it does not support booking.
- Each resort runs its own booking and rental system, operated by phone and fax.
- Each resort processes payments semi-manually.
- Data is stored in a separate local database at each resort.
- Assistants and accountants at each resort handle operations manually, and this approach no longer scales.

### 1.2 Business Goals
- **BG-1:** Expand the customer base.
- **BG-2:** Modernize the online presence and let customers book and rent online.
- **BG-3:** Consolidate all bookings, rentals and accounting into one central system.
- **BG-4:** Automate as much as possible so staff can focus on customers and the business.
- **BG-5:** Support marketing through social media, travel agencies and tourism networks.
- **BG-6:** Improve services through customer feedback.
- **BG-7:** Allow future expansion to more resorts and non-ski services.

### 1.3 Scope
**In scope (initial release):**
- The three existing resorts.
- Existing services: rooms, equipment and clothing rental, and ski classes.
- The public website, booking, payment, back-office operations, reporting, promotions and feedback.

**Out of scope (initial release), but the design must accommodate them:**
- Additional resorts.
- Non-ski services such as hiking trips.
- A loyalty program.
- Virtual tours.
- A customer messaging service.

### 1.4 Priority Legend
- **M (Must):** explicitly required by the stakeholder.
- **S (Should):** clearly desired by the stakeholder.
- **C (Could):** optional or nice-to-have.
- **F (Future):** design for extensibility only; not built in the initial release.
- **(derived):** not stated explicitly, but needed to fulfil a stated need. These items require stakeholder confirmation.

## 2. Users and Stakeholders

| Role | Description |
|---|---|
| **Customer / Visitor** | Browses information, books and pays for services, gives feedback. Uses mobile and desktop devices. |
| **Resort Staff** | Handles exceptions, walk-in check-ins, manual corrections and customer feedback alerts. |
| **Accounting Staff** | Generates and reviews accounting reports and corrects accounting entries. |
| **Owner / Management** | Views reports across all resorts, manages promotions and defines report templates. |
| **System Administrator** (derived) | Manages user accounts, roles, configuration and content. |
| **External Partners** | Travel agencies, tourism networks, equipment suppliers, social media platforms, and weather and lift data providers. |
| **Beta Testers** | The owner, selected employees and selected customers. |

## 3. Functional Requirements

### 3.1 Public Website and Information

**FR-1.1 (M)** The system shall provide a single public website covering all three resorts.
- It shall present information about each resort, its services and contact details.

**FR-1.2 (M)** The website shall display each resort's services, including rooms, equipment and clothing rental, and ski classes.

**FR-1.3 (S)** The website shall display high-quality images of each resort.
- A virtual tour is deferred (see FR-14.2).

**FR-1.4 (S)** The website shall display live weather and snow conditions for each resort.
- The data shall come from an external weather service.

**FR-1.5 (S)** The website shall display real-time ski lift operating status and wait times for each resort.
- The data shall come from existing lift systems, if available. See Open Issue OI-5.

**FR-1.6 (S)** The website shall provide an events calendar for each resort.
- The calendar shall show special events and seasonal activities.
- Authorized staff shall be able to manage calendar entries.

**FR-1.7 (S)** The website shall provide a help section and/or tutorials for customers.

### 3.2 Customer Accounts (derived)

**FR-2.1 (S, derived)** Customers shall be able to create an account and log in.
- Accounts are needed to manage bookings, view history, submit feedback and receive personalized recommendations.

**FR-2.2 (S, derived)** Customers shall be able to view their current and past bookings and rentals.

**FR-2.3 (C, derived)** Customers shall be able to book as a guest without creating an account.
- This requires stakeholder confirmation.

### 3.3 Online Booking and Rental

**FR-3.1 (M)** Customers shall be able to book rooms online at any of the three resorts.

**FR-3.2 (M)** Customers shall be able to rent ski equipment and clothing online at any of the three resorts.

**FR-3.3 (M)** Customers shall be able to book ski training classes online at any of the three resorts.

**FR-3.4 (M)** The system shall show real-time availability of rooms, equipment, clothing and class places before a booking is confirmed.

**FR-3.5 (S, derived)** Customers shall be able to combine several services into a single booking.
- For example, a room, equipment and a class in one booking.

**FR-3.6 (M)** The system shall automatically confirm a booking or rental when no problem is detected.
- Examples of problems: no availability, payment failure or conflicting data.

**FR-3.7 (M)** The system shall flag any booking or rental with a problem for review by resort staff.
- The system shall notify the responsible staff.

**FR-3.8 (S, derived)** The system shall send customers an automatic confirmation of each booking.
- The confirmation shall be sent by email.

**FR-3.9 (S, derived)** Customers shall be able to modify or cancel bookings, according to configurable policies.
- The policies need to be defined.

### 3.4 Inventory and Equipment Management

**FR-4.1 (M)** The system shall track each resort's inventory of equipment and clothing.
- It shall track the total quantity, the quantity available and the quantity currently rented out.

**FR-4.2 (M)** The system shall update inventory automatically when items are rented, returned or reserved.

**FR-4.3 (S)** The system shall provide an interface to external equipment suppliers or rental services.
- This allows items to be sourced when the resort's own stock is insufficient.

**FR-4.4 (S, derived)** Staff shall be able to record equipment returns and the condition of returned items.

### 3.5 Back-Office and Manual Operations

**FR-5.1 (M)** Authorized staff shall be able to create, edit and delete bookings, rentals and accounting entries manually.
- This supports correcting errors.

**FR-5.2 (M)** Staff shall be able to check in customers who did not book online, such as walk-ins or phone bookings.
- These bookings shall be recorded in the same central system.

**FR-5.3 (M)** The system shall record an audit trail for all manual changes.
- The audit trail shall include who made the change, when, and the old and new values.
- (Derived; needed for accounting integrity.)

**FR-5.4 (M)** The system shall restrict functions according to user role.
- Roles include customer, staff, accounting, management and administrator.

**FR-5.5 (S)** The system shall provide a staff dashboard showing current bookings, flagged exceptions, inventory status and open feedback items.

### 3.6 Payments

**FR-6.1 (M)** The system shall accept online payment for bookings and rentals.

**FR-6.2 (M)** The system shall support common payment methods.
- Examples: major credit and debit cards and popular digital wallets.
- The exact list is to be confirmed.

**FR-6.3 (M)** The system shall process payments through a secure, certified payment gateway.

**FR-6.4 (M)** Staff shall be able to record payments taken on-site for walk-in and manual bookings.

**FR-6.5 (S, derived)** The system shall support refunds according to the cancellation policy.

### 3.7 Central Data Management

**FR-7.1 (M)** All bookings, rentals, inventory, payments and accounting data for all resorts shall be stored in one central database.

**FR-7.2 (M)** All data shall be identifiable by resort.
- This enables both per-resort and cross-resort views.

**FR-7.3 (M)** Existing data from the three local resort databases shall be migrated into the central database.
- (Derived.)

### 3.8 Reporting

**FR-8.1 (M)** The system shall generate accounting reports automatically at configurable times.
- Examples: daily, weekly or monthly.

**FR-8.2 (M)** Authorized users shall be able to generate accounting reports on demand.

**FR-8.3 (M)** Reports shall be available for each resort individually and for all resorts combined.

**FR-8.4 (S, derived)** Reports shall be exportable in common formats, such as PDF and spreadsheet.

### 3.9 Promotions, Marketing and Partner Integration

**FR-9.1 (M)** Management shall be able to create and manage promotions.
- Examples: discounts and special offers with a defined start and end date.

**FR-9.2 (M)** The system shall integrate with social media platforms so promotions and advertisements can be published easily.
- The specific platforms are to be confirmed.

**FR-9.3 (S)** The system shall support joint promotions with travel agencies and participation in tourism-network promotion events.

**FR-9.4 (S)** The system shall support integration with external travel and tourism platforms.
- Examples: travel agencies and online travel booking sites.

**FR-9.5 (C)** Customers shall be able to share resort content or promotions on social media.

### 3.10 Promotion Analytics

**FR-10.1 (M)** The system shall track bookings attributed to each promotion.
- It shall compare bookings during the promotion against a non-promotion baseline.

**FR-10.2 (S)** The system shall measure the reach of a promotion.
- Examples: impressions, clicks and visits from social media or partner channels.

**FR-10.3 (S)** The system shall measure the reception of a promotion.
- Examples: engagement and customer feedback related to the promotion.

**FR-10.4 (S)** Management shall be able to define report templates for promotions.

**FR-10.5 (S)** The system shall automatically generate a promotion report from the selected template when a promotion ends.

### 3.11 Customer Feedback, Reviews and Recommendations

**FR-11.1 (S)** Customers shall be able to submit reviews of resorts and services.

**FR-11.2 (S)** The system shall offer an optional feedback survey after a stay.
- The survey shall be offered at checkout and/or by a follow-up email.

**FR-11.3 (M)** Feedback requests shall be non-intrusive.
- The system shall limit how often a customer is asked for feedback.
- Customers shall be able to opt out.

**FR-11.4 (S)** Customers shall be able to submit instant feedback at any time during their stay.
- Example: "The equipment wasn't optimal."

**FR-11.5 (S)** With instant feedback, customers shall be able to request a direct remedy.
- Example: a replacement for faulty equipment.

**FR-11.6 (S)** Customers shall be able to submit suggestions for future features or services through the feedback system.

**FR-11.7 (S)** The system shall provide personalized recommendations to customers.
- Recommendations shall be based on booking history, preferences and behaviour.

### 3.12 Staff Feedback Alerts and Handling

**FR-12.1 (S)** The system shall alert available staff about new instant feedback and issue reports.
- Alerts shall be sent by email and/or shown on a staff dashboard.

**FR-12.2 (S, derived)** A staff member shall be able to claim a feedback item, record the action taken and mark it resolved.
- This prevents several staff members handling the same item.

**FR-12.3 (S)** The system shall suggest possible actions to staff for each type of feedback or issue.
- These suggestions come from a maintainable guideline and resource section.

**FR-12.4 (C, derived)** The system shall escalate a feedback item that is not handled within a configurable time.

### 3.13 Staff Help

**FR-13.1 (S)** The system shall provide help documentation and tutorials for staff.
- These complement the onboarding sessions.

### 3.14 Future and Optional Features (design for extensibility)

**FR-14.1 (F)** Loyalty program.

**FR-14.2 (F)** Virtual tours of the resorts.

**FR-14.3 (F)** Messaging and notification service.
- This will be decided based on customer demand after launch.

**FR-14.4 (F)** Additional resorts.

**FR-14.5 (F)** Additional services, including non-ski services such as hiking trips.

## 4. Non-Functional Requirements

### 4.1 Usability and Design

**NFR-1.1 (M)** The user interface shall be modern and aesthetically pleasing.

**NFR-1.2 (M)** The system shall be easy to use for customers, staff and the owner.
- This includes intuitive navigation and clear instructions.

**NFR-1.3 (M)** The customer-facing system shall be responsive.
- It shall work on mobile devices and desktop computers.

**NFR-1.4 (S, derived)** The website should meet common web accessibility standards, such as WCAG 2.1 AA.

### 4.2 Security and Privacy

**NFR-2.1 (M)** Customer data shall be protected using industry best practices.
- This includes encryption in transit and at rest, access control and secure authentication.

**NFR-2.2 (M)** The system shall be protected against hacking and service disruption.
- This includes protection against common web attacks and denial-of-service.

**NFR-2.3 (M)** Payment processing shall comply with PCI DSS.

**NFR-2.4 (M)** The system shall comply with all applicable industry and privacy regulations.
- Examples: US state privacy laws such as CCPA/CPRA, and GDPR where EU customers are served.
- The applicable regulations must be identified (see OI-10).

**NFR-2.5 (S, derived)** Data shall be backed up regularly, with a defined recovery procedure.

### 4.3 Reliability and Availability

**NFR-3.1 (M)** The system shall be highly available, especially during the ski season.
- Target uptime is to be defined.

**NFR-3.2 (S, derived)** Staff shall be able to continue essential on-site operations, such as check-in and rentals, during outages of external services.
- External services include weather, lift data and social media.

### 4.4 Scalability, Modularity and Extensibility

**NFR-4.1 (M)** The architecture shall be modular.
- New resorts and new service types can then be added through configuration or extension, without redesign.

**NFR-4.2 (M)** The system shall allow new features to be added later without major rework.
- Examples: the loyalty program, virtual tours and messaging.

**NFR-4.3 (S)** The system shall scale to handle growth in customers and bookings.

### 4.5 Integration

**NFR-5.1 (M)** The system shall provide well-defined interfaces to all external systems, such as APIs.
- Payment gateways.
- Social media platforms.
- Weather services.
- Ski lift systems.
- Travel and tourism partners.
- Equipment suppliers.

### 4.6 Performance (to be quantified)

**NFR-6.1 (S)** Availability checks and booking confirmation shall complete within an acceptable response time.
- The target is to be defined, for example under 3 seconds.

**NFR-6.2 (S)** Live data shall be refreshed at a defined interval.
- This applies to weather, snow and lift data.

## 5. Implementation and Transition Constraints

**TC-1 (M)** Implementation and rollout shall not interrupt day-to-day business at any resort.
- This may require running the old and new systems in parallel and migrating outside peak periods.

**TC-2 (M)** The system shall be tested before general go-live by the owner, selected employees and selected customers.
- This shall be done through a beta or phased rollout.

**TC-3 (S)** The rollout should be phased, for example resort by resort or feature by feature.
- Feedback from each phase should be incorporated before full launch.

**TC-4 (M)** Staff shall receive onboarding sessions before using the system.

**TC-5 (M)** Data from the existing local databases shall be migrated accurately before cut-over.
