# Software Requirements: Cool Ski Resorts Booking & Management System

## 1. Context and Scope

**Business:** Cool Ski Resorts is a chain of **three ski resorts** on different mountains in the USA. Each resort offers:
- Resort hotel accommodation
- Ski rental
- Ski lessons/training

**Business goals stated by the stakeholder:**
- **BG-1:** Give customers a pleasant, smooth stay at the resorts.
- **BG-2:** Increase the company's visibility and reach more customers.
- **BG-3:** Fully digitize booking and administration, and replace fax- and phone-based processes.

**Scope of this document:**
- The main software system is a **new, centralized booking and management system** (Section 3–5).
- The interview also covered a social-media initiative and staff training. These are mostly *not* software requirements. They are recorded separately (Sections 6–7) so they are not lost.

**Legend:**
- **[S] Stated:** the stakeholder said this explicitly.
- **[D] Derived:** inferred by the reviewer from stated needs. Must be confirmed.
- **Priority:** M = Must, S = Should, C = Could (MoSCoW).

## 7. Open Issues and Questions for Follow-up

The interview ended early, and several important points were not covered.

| # | Topic | Question |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Budget | No budget was given for the system, the agency or training. What are the budget limits? |
| Q2 | Timeline | "This year" was said about social media. Does the same deadline apply to the booking/management system? |
| Q3 | Inventory scope | What does "all inventory" include? Rooms, rental equipment and lesson slots only, or also lift passes, consumables, maintenance and staff scheduling? |
| Q4 | Lift tickets | Should ski passes and lift tickets be bookable? They were not mentioned. |
| Q5 | Data security | The data security and privacy question was not answered. Are there specific requirements or concerns? |
| Q6 | Integrations | Are there existing systems (accounting, POS, lift access, website) the new system must connect to? |
| Q7 | Build vs. buy | Does "completely new solution" mean custom development or a new commercial product? |
| Q8 | Customer accounts | Should customers have accounts, a booking history or loyalty features? |
| Q9 | Social platforms | "Ja kann ich mir gut vorstellen" did not specify platforms or content types. Which ones should be prioritized? |
| Q10 | Target audience | Which customer groups or markets should social media target? This was asked but not answered. |
| Q11 | Stakeholder role | What is the interviewee's role, and who else (resort managers, front-desk staff, ski school, IT) should be interviewed? |
| Q12 | Pricing rules | Are there seasonal pricing, packages, discounts or group bookings the system must support? |
| Q13 | Reporting | Which reports or KPIs does management need? |

## 8. Reviewer Notes on the Interview

- **Mixed project scope:** The interview covered two separate initiatives: marketing/social media and a booking/management system. The software requirements come almost entirely from the second part, which got relatively little time.
- **Compound questions:** The interviewer often asked two or three questions at once. The stakeholder usually answered only one of them, so budget, security, target groups and platform choice remained open.
- **Most requirements are derived:** Only about 12 requirements are explicitly stated. The rest are reasonable inferences and should be validated in a follow-up session.
- **Recommendation:** Schedule a second interview focused only on the booking/management system, with Q1–Q8 and Q11–Q13 as the agenda. Include operational staff from at least one resort.
