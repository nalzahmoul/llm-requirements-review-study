# Software Requirements Specification (SRS)
**Project:** Cool Ski Resorts Digitalization Platform
**Stakeholder:** Mary (Owner)

## 1. Project Overview & Business Goals
The objective of this project is to replace the current static website and semi-manual (telephone/fax) local processes with a centralized, web-based platform for "Cool Ski Resorts" (comprising 3 resort locations). 
*   **Primary Goals:** Streamline business operations, centralize data across all three resorts, automate financial reporting, and increase revenue by expanding market reach across the US.

## 2. User Roles
*   **Customers:** End-users who browse the website, register accounts, and book accommodations, classes, and equipment.
*   **Owner (Mary):** Super-admin with full access to centralized data, financial reports, and system configurations.
*   **Assistants:** Administrative users responsible for generating reports and performing ongoing website maintenance.

## 3. Functional Requirements (FR)

### 3.1. Centralized Database & Architecture (Priority: High)
*   **FR-1.1:** The system shall utilize a single, centralized database to track reservations, bookings, equipment hires, and inventory across all three resort locations.
*   **FR-1.2:** The system shall allow users (customers and staff) to filter or select specific resort locations when viewing availability or data.

### 3.2. Online Booking, Reservations, and Hires (Priority: High)
*   **FR-2.1:** The system shall allow customers to make resort stay reservations online.
*   **FR-2.2:** The system shall allow customers to book ski classes online.
*   **FR-2.3:** The system shall allow customers to hire (rent) ski equipment in advance online, directly integrated with the resort's real-time inventory.
*   **FR-2.4:** The system shall generate a digital confirmation/receipt that customers can present on-site upon arrival.

### 3.3. User Account Management
*   **FR-3.1:** The system shall allow customers to register for an account and log in using an email address and password.
*   **FR-3.2:** Logged-in customers shall be able to view and track their current and past reservations, class bookings, and equipment hires.

### 3.4. Payment Processing (Priority: High)
*   **FR-4.1:** The system shall integrate a third-party payment gateway to securely process online payments for reservations, classes, and equipment hires at the time of booking.

### 3.5. Inventory Management
*   **FR-5.1:** The system shall track the real-time availability of rental ski equipment across all three resorts.
*   **FR-5.2:** The system shall log the rental history of inventory items.
*   **FR-5.3:** The system shall track maintenance schedules for ski equipment.
*   **FR-5.4:** The system shall generate automated alerts for staff when inventory stock is low or when specific equipment requires maintenance/servicing.

### 3.6. Reporting & Financials
*   **FR-6.1:** The system shall generate financial reports detailing revenue and expenses related to bookings, reservations, and hires.
*   **FR-6.2:** The system shall support reporting necessary for accounting and taxation purposes.
*   **FR-6.3:** Reports shall be accessible by the Owner and authorized Assistants, with the ability to view data per resort or aggregated across all three resorts.

### 3.7. Customer Engagement & External Integrations (Priority: Low/Medium)
*   **FR-7.1:** The website shall display weather forecasts/updates for the resort locations, specifically showing customers the expected weather during their booked stay.
*   **FR-7.2:** The website shall include features to support social media promotion (e.g., social sharing buttons, links to resort social profiles).
*   **FR-7.3:** The system shall include a Content Management System (CMS) or admin portal allowing Assistants to maintain and update website content.

## 4. Non-Functional Requirements (NFR)
*   **NFR-1 (Security):** The system shall implement industry-standard security protocols (e.g., HTTPS, encrypted passwords, PCI-DSS compliance via the payment gateway) to protect user data and financial transactions.
*   **NFR-2 (Usability):** The public-facing website shall be modern, visually appealing, and intuitive for customers across the US to navigate.
*   **NFR-3 (Availability/Reliability):** The centralized database must update in real-time to prevent double-booking of rooms, classes, or equipment across the resorts.

## 5. Project & Transition Requirements
*   **TR-1 (Business Continuity):** The deployment of the new system must be executed with minimal disruption to the current daily operations of the resorts.
*   **TR-2 (Training):** Training and support documentation must be provided to the Assistants to transition them from manual report generation to using the new reporting tools and maintaining the website.

## 6. Reviewer’s Notes: Ambiguities & Missing Requirements
*The following areas were glossed over during the interview and require follow-up elicitation with Mary or her assistants before development begins:*

1.  **Data Migration:** Mary mentioned existing local databases at each of the three resorts. Do we need to migrate historical data and future bookings from those local databases into the new centralized system?
2.  **Modification/Cancellation Policies:** How should the system handle booking cancellations, refunds, or date modifications by the customer?
3.  **On-Site Walk-ins:** The system handles *online* bookings, but how will staff handle walk-in customers at the resort? Will front-desk staff use the same web portal to input walk-in rentals so inventory remains accurate?
4.  **Expense Tracking:** Mary agreed to a feature tracking "revenue and expenses." While the system will naturally track revenue via the payment gateway, how are "expenses" inputted into this system?
5.  **Taxation Specifics:** Are the three resorts in different states or municipalities with different tax rates? The payment gateway and reporting module will need specific tax rules configured.
