### 1. Project Overview & Business Context
* **Client:** Cool Ski Resorts
* **Locations:** 3 ski resorts located in various mountain ranges in the USA.
* **Core Services:** Resort hotels, ski equipment rental, and ski training/lessons.
* **Business Goals:** 
  1. Provide a smooth and pleasant stay for customers.
  2. Increase company visibility and reach more customers via Social Media.
  3. Completely digitize the booking and management processes, replacing outdated methods (phone and fax) with a brand-new, centralized system.

---

### 2. Functional Software Requirements (Booking & Management System)
The stakeholder wants a completely new software solution. Based on the services they offer and their stated needs, the system must include the following functionalities:

* **FR1: Online Reservation System**
  * **FR1.1:** Customers must be able to book resort hotel rooms online.
  * **FR1.2:** Customers must be able to reserve ski rental equipment online.
  * **FR1.3:** Customers must be able to book ski training/lessons online.
  * **FR1.4:** Staff must be able to view and manage all reservations digitally (replacing phone/fax workflows).
* **FR2: Online Payment Processing**
  * **FR2.1:** The system must allow customers to pay for their reservations (hotel, rentals, lessons) online.
* **FR3: Digital Inventory Management**
  * **FR3.1:** The system must track and manage all resort inventory digitally (e.g., available hotel rooms, ski rental equipment stock, and ski instructor availability).
  * **FR3.2:** Inventory should automatically update when an online reservation is made.

---

### 3. Non-Functional & Architectural Requirements
* **NFR1: Centralized Database**
  * The system must utilize a single, centralized database that connects and manages data across all three US ski resort locations.
* **NFR2: System Deployment**
  * The system must be a completely new implementation (greenfield project or new Commercial Off-The-Shelf software), as there is no existing digital legacy system to integrate with.

---

### 4. Project, Training, & Marketing Requirements (Non-Software)
A significant portion of the interview covered operational rollout and marketing. While not software features, these are critical project requirements for the stakeholder:

* **PR1: System Training (Rollout)**
  * **PR1.1:** Training for the new booking/management software must be conducted by an external training provider.
  * **PR1.2:** Training must be conducted on-site (Vor-Ort-Training) at the resorts.
  * **PR1.3:** Training must be executed in phases/batches (staffelweise) so that daily resort operations are not disrupted.
* **PR2: Social Media & Marketing**
  * **PR2.1:** Procure an external Social Media Agency to build an online presence from scratch (considering platforms like Instagram, Facebook, TikTok).
  * **PR2.2:** The agency selection criteria must prioritize expertise and innovative ideas over the size of the agency.
  * **PR2.3:** The agency must train internal resort staff on how to manage social media.
  * **PR2.4:** The social media implementation and staff training must be completed within the current calendar year.

### 5. Reviewer’s Notes: Missing Information & Next Steps
As a requirements reviewer, I must point out that the interviewer spent too much time on the Social Media aspect and ran out of time before gathering deep technical requirements for the software. **A follow-up session is required to clarify the following gaps:**

1. **Multi-Location Logic:** Can a customer book across multiple resorts in one transaction, or are they separate portals connected to one central database?
2. **Inventory Specifics:** What specific metrics are needed for ski rentals (e.g., tracking boot sizes, ski lengths, maintenance status/damaged gear)?
3. **Payment Gateways:** Which payment methods (Credit Card, PayPal, etc.) and currencies (USD only, or international?) must be supported?
4. **Cancellation/Modification:** What are the business rules for customers canceling or changing a booking online?
5. **User Roles:** What are the different staff permission levels needed for the management system (e.g., Front Desk vs. Ski Rental Tech vs. Resort Manager)?
6. **Compliance:** Since the resorts are in the USA, the system will need to comply with US data privacy laws and PCI-DSS (for payments), though the stakeholder seems to be German-speaking (potential GDPR implications if they market to European tourists).
