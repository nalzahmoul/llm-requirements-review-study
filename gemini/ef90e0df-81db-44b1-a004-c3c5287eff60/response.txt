# Software Requirements Specification: Cool Ski Resorts Digitalization

## 1. Project Overview
The objective of this project is to digitalize and centralize the booking and management systems across all hotel entities owned by "Cool Ski Resorts." Additionally, the project aims to modernize the resort's marketing efforts by automating social media management and transitioning the current static website into a dynamic platform that integrates social media content and live weather updates.

## 2. Stakeholders
*   **Primary:** Resort Owners, Hotel Employees, Customers, Software Developers.
*   **Secondary:** External Tool Providers, Social Media Platforms.

## 3. Prioritized Project Phases
1.  **Phase 1:** Identify and standardize shared processes across hotel entities (Reservations, Customer Service, Payments).
2.  **Phase 2:** Digital implementation of the centralized booking and management system.
3.  **Phase 3:** Implementation of social media automation.
4.  **Phase 4:** Website integration (Social media blog feed and live weather).

---

## 4. Functional Requirements

### 4.1. Centralized Booking & Management System (Phases 1 & 2)
*   **FR-1.1 Centralized Database:** The system shall utilize a single, centralized database to manage customer data, room inventory, and bookings across all hotel entities.
*   **FR-1.2 Standardized Processes:** The system shall standardize and share workflows for reservations, customer service, and payment processing across all hotel locations.
*   **FR-1.3 Room Availability & Pricing:** The system shall display real-time room availability and corresponding pricing to both customers and employees.
*   **FR-1.4 Dynamic Promotions & Highlighting:** The system shall allow administrators/employees to highlight specific dates and apply discounts to periods with low demand.
*   **FR-1.5 Search Analytics (Customer Insights):** The system shall track, log, and report on what potential customers are searching for (e.g., dates, room types) within the booking engine.
*   **FR-1.6 Payment Processing:** The system shall process customer payments securely via a third-party payment gateway (e.g., Stripe).

### 4.2. Social Media Automation (Phase 3)
*   **FR-2.1 Multi-Platform Integration:** The system shall connect to as many major social media platforms as possible to maximize brand visibility.
*   **FR-2.2 Post Scheduling:** The system shall allow users to plan and schedule social media posts (text and images) in advance.
*   **FR-2.3 Automated Publishing:** The system shall automatically publish scheduled posts across all connected social media platforms at the designated times.
*   **FR-2.4 Audience Insights:** The social media tool shall provide analytics and insights regarding current customers and interested prospects to aid in acquiring new customer segments and brand building.

### 4.3. Website Integration & Content Management (Phase 4)
*   **FR-3.1 Headless CMS Integration:** The system shall use a Headless CMS (e.g., Contentful) as a single source of truth to store post data (text and images) and streamline it to both the social media automation tool and the resort’s website.
*   **FR-3.2 Dynamic Blog Experience:** The formerly static website shall dynamically display the social media posts (including images) in a blog-like feed.
*   **FR-3.3 Live Weather Display:** The website shall integrate with an external Weather API to display current weather conditions at the resort.

---

## 5. Non-Functional Requirements & Constraints

### 5.1. Architecture & Procurement Constraints (Buy vs. Build)
*   **NFR-1.1 Off-the-Shelf/Open Source Preference:** The booking, customer service, and CMS systems must not be built from scratch. The project shall utilize existing open-source solutions or commercial systems where the source code can be purchased.
*   **NFR-1.2 Modularity & Footprint:** The chosen software systems shall be as lightweight ("small") as possible to minimize the time and cost required for future adjustments or new feature implementations.
*   **NFR-1.3 Customizability:** The acquired source code must be highly adjustable to allow developers to tailor the system to the resort's specific processes.

### 5.2. Usability
*   **NFR-2.1 Simplicity:** Both the customer-facing interfaces and employee back-office interfaces must be extremely simple and intuitive.
*   **NFR-2.2 Training Efficiency:** The employee interface must be easy to learn to minimize onboarding time and reduce training costs for new staff.

### 5.3. Financial & Cost Constraints
*   **NFR-3.1 Payment Gateway Cost-Efficiency:** The transaction fees of the selected third-party payment provider (e.g., Stripe) over a 10-year operational period must not exceed the projected total cost of building and maintaining a self-implemented payment solution (to be evaluated using a Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) method or similar financial model).

---

## 6. Success Criteria
The project will be deemed successful when:
1.  All standardized processes (reservations, customer service, payments, and marketing automation) are fully operational across all hotel entities.
2.  Key stakeholders (owners, employees, customers, and developers) express satisfaction with the usability and performance of the new systems.

## 7. Reviewer Notes / Areas for Further Clarification
*As a requirements reviewer, I recommend following up with the stakeholder on the following ambiguities before development begins:*
*   **Customer Service Scope:** The stakeholder mentioned standardizing "customer service," but no specific functional requirements were gathered for this (e.g., Is a ticketing system, live chat, or CRM required?).
*   **Specific Social Platforms:** "As many platforms as possible" needs to be explicitly defined (e.g., Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, X/Twitter, LinkedIn) to ensure the chosen automation tool supports the required APIs.
*   **Legacy Data Migration:** It is unclear if existing customer/booking data from the decentralized hotel systems needs to be migrated into the new centralized database.
