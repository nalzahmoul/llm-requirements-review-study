# Software Requirements Specification (SRS)
**Project:** Cool Ski Resorts Online Booking and Management System  
**Stakeholder:** Jim Smith (Owner, Cool Ski Resorts)

## 1. Project Overview & Business Goals
Cool Ski Resorts, a chain of three U.S.-based ski resorts, currently relies on manual, localized processes (phone/fax bookings, on-site manual payments, and isolated databases). The goal of this project is to modernize operations by developing a centralized, web-based system for reservations, bookings, inventory, and payments. This system aims to improve customer experience, reduce manual errors, unify operations across all three resorts, and increase market visibility.

---

## 2. Prioritized Functional Requirements

The stakeholder explicitly prioritized the core features in the following implementation order:

### Priority 1: Online Reservations and Bookings (Core)
*   **FR-1.1:** The system shall provide a self-service web interface allowing customers to book hotel rooms across any of the three resorts.
*   **FR-1.2:** The system shall allow customers to rent ski equipment online.
*   **FR-1.3:** The system shall allow customers to register for ski training classes online.
*   **FR-1.4:** The system shall automatically generate and send a booking confirmation email to the customer upon completing a reservation.
*   **FR-1.5:** The confirmation email shall include a unique booking reference number and a scannable QR code.
*   **FR-1.6:** The system shall provide an interface/capability for resort staff to scan the customer's QR code using smartphones to streamline the on-site check-in/entry process.

### Priority 2: Centralized Database (Core)
*   **FR-2.1:** The system shall utilize a single, unified database that connects all three resort locations.
*   **FR-2.2:** The database shall synchronize and store customer data, reservation details, inventory levels, and financial/taxation records in real time.
*   **FR-2.3:** The system shall allow staff and management to view and coordinate operations across all three resorts from a centralized source.

### Priority 3: Integrated Payment System (Core)
*   **FR-3.1:** The system shall allow customers to pay for reservations, rentals, and classes online at the time of booking.
*   **FR-3.2:** The system shall support both partial payments (booking deposits) and full payments.
*   **FR-3.3:** The system shall automatically log online payment details into the centralized database, eliminating the need for staff to manually input payment records upon guest arrival/departure.

### Priority 4: Inventory Management (Core)
*   **FR-4.1:** The system shall track room occupancy in real time across all three resorts.
*   **FR-4.2:** The system shall track the availability of rental ski equipment and training class slots in real time.
*   **FR-4.3:** The system shall automatically update inventory upon booking to prevent overbooking of rooms or shortages of equipment.

### Priority 5: Social Media Integration (Enhancement)
*   **FR-5.1:** The website shall integrate with and provide links to the resorts' social media platforms, specifically Facebook, Instagram, and Twitter (X), to facilitate marketing and guest interaction.

### Priority 6: Weather and Snow Conditions (Enhancement)
*   **FR-6.1:** The website shall display real-time weather forecasts and snow conditions for the resorts to assist guests in planning their trips.

## 3. Future / Phase 2 Requirements (Architectural Consideration)
*Note: The stakeholder requested that these features not be prioritized for immediate release, but the system must be designed with the architectural flexibility to easily add them later.*

*   **FR-7.1 (Customer Reviews):** The system architecture shall accommodate a future module for guests to leave public feedback, reviews, and testimonials.
*   **FR-7.2 (Loyalty Program):** The database and user management system shall be extensible to support a repeat-customer rewards and discount program in the future.
*   **FR-7.3 (Mobile Optimization):** The web application should be designed to allow for full mobile responsiveness so customers can easily book via smartphones.

---

## 4. Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs)

*   **NFR-1 (Availability):** The online booking system shall be available to customers 24/7.
*   **NFR-2 (Usability):** The customer-facing booking and payment workflows must be simple, intuitive, and have high usability to avoid overcomplicating the process for guests.
*   **NFR-3 (Security):** The integrated payment system must process transactions securely (e.g., PCI-DSS compliance).
*   **NFR-4 (Maintainability/Scalability):** The system architecture must be modular to allow the seamless addition of Phase 2 features (Loyalty, Reviews) without requiring a system rewrite.
*   **NFR-5 (Transition/Deployment):** The rollout of the new system must cause minimal disruption to the resorts' current day-to-day operations.
