# Software Requirements Specification: Cool Ski Resorts Digital Management System

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Purpose
This document states the software requirements gathered from the elicitation interview with Mary Smith, owner of Cool Ski Resorts. Each requirement is labeled as **Stated** (said directly by the stakeholder) or **Derived** (inferred from the discussion and needing confirmation).

### 1.2 Background
Cool Ski Resorts runs three ski resorts on different mountains in the US. Each resort offers:
- Hotel room bookings
- Ski equipment and clothing hire
- Ski training classes

### 1.3 Current Situation
- Customers make reservations by telephone or fax.
- Each resort handles bookings semi-manually in its own local database.
- Assistants track finances separately.
- Resorts cannot see each other's data.

### 1.4 Project Goal
Digitalize all resort operations with minimal disruption. The new system will provide:
- An online customer portal
- A shared database across all three resorts
- A customer-facing website with marketing features

### 1.5 Priorities (Stated by Stakeholder)

| Priority | Feature Area |
|---|---|
| **High** | Online customer portal |
| **High** | Access to all resort data from every resort (centralized database) |
| **High** | Digitalization of all operations |
| **Lower** | Attractive customer website design |
| **Lower** | Weather forecast on website |
| **Lower** | Social media promotion |

## 3. Functional Requirements

### 3.1 Customer Portal (Priority: High)

| ID | Requirement | Source |
|---|---|---|
| FR-CP-01 | The system shall provide an online portal where customers can make hotel room reservations at any of the three resorts. | Stated |
| FR-CP-02 | The portal shall allow customers to book ski training classes. | Stated |
| FR-CP-03 | The portal shall allow customers to book ski equipment and clothing hire. | Stated |
| FR-CP-04 | The portal shall allow customers to view and manage their existing reservations and bookings. | Stated (confirmed as "necessary") |
| FR-CP-05 | The portal shall show real-time availability of rooms, class places, and hire inventory before a customer confirms a booking. | Derived (from the live-update requirement) |
| FR-CP-06 | The system shall send customers a confirmation for each booking made or changed. | Derived |
| FR-CP-07 | The portal shall let customers create an account or otherwise identify themselves so they can retrieve and manage their bookings. | Derived |
| FR-CP-08 | Customers shall be able to make several bookings (room, class, hire) for the same stay. | Derived |

*Note: The stakeholder delegated the detailed portal workflow and user experience to the development team ("that's up to you").*

### 3.2 Centralized Resort Data (Priority: High)

| ID | Requirement | Source |
|---|---|---|
| FR-DB-01 | The system shall keep a single central database holding data for all three resorts. | Stated |
| FR-DB-02 | The database shall record hotel reservations for each resort. | Stated |
| FR-DB-03 | The database shall record class bookings for each resort. | Stated |
| FR-DB-04 | The database shall record equipment and clothing hires for each resort. | Stated |
| FR-DB-05 | The database shall track the equipment and clothing inventory at each resort. | Stated |
| FR-DB-06 | The database shall record payment data for all reservations, bookings, and hires. | Stated |
| FR-DB-07 | Authorized users at any resort shall be able to access the data of every resort. | Stated |
| FR-DB-08 | Changes made at any resort or through the customer portal shall appear immediately (live) to all users at all resorts. | Stated |
| FR-DB-09 | The system shall provide reports or views of payment data, filterable by resort. | Stated (payment data requested); report details Derived |

### 3.3 Access Control

| ID | Requirement | Source |
|---|---|---|
| FR-AC-01 | All staff users shall have read-only access to the data of all resorts. | Stated |
| FR-AC-02 | Only the Resort Lead shall be able to create, edit, or delete data for their resort, except as allowed in FR-AC-03. | Stated (limiting edits to the lead's own resort is Derived) |
| FR-AC-03 | Receptionists shall be able to create, edit, and cancel bookings (room reservations, class bookings, hire bookings). | Stated |
| FR-AC-04 | Receptionists shall not be able to edit non-booking data such as inventory or payment records. | Derived |
| FR-AC-05 | All staff shall log in with individual credentials, and the system shall enforce permissions based on role. | Derived |
| FR-AC-06 | The system shall record who changed what and when (audit trail). | Derived |

### 3.4 Digitalization of Operations (Priority: High)

| ID | Requirement | Source |
|---|---|---|
| FR-OP-01 | All reservation, booking, hire, inventory, and financial processes shall be handled digitally in the system. | Stated ("everything should be digital") |
| FR-OP-02 | Staff shall be able to enter bookings received by telephone, or in person, directly into the system. | Derived |
| FR-OP-03 | The system shall replace manual financial tracking by recording payments digitally and linking them to their bookings. | Derived (from current assistant-based process) |
| FR-OP-04 | The system shall support migrating existing data from each resort's local database. | Derived (needed for non-disruptive transition) |
| FR-OP-05 | Staff shall be able to record equipment hire-out and return, updating inventory automatically. | Derived |

### 3.5 Customer Website and Marketing (Priority: Lower)

| ID | Requirement | Source |
|---|---|---|
| FR-WEB-01 | The system shall include a visually appealing public website for customers, presenting all three resorts. | Stated |
| FR-WEB-02 | The website shall show a weather forecast for each resort location. | Stated |
| FR-WEB-03 | The website shall support social media promotion, such as links to social media accounts and sharing features. | Stated (specific mechanism Derived) |
| FR-WEB-04 | The website shall give customers direct access to the customer portal. | Derived |

## 5. Implementation Constraints

| ID | Constraint | Source |
|---|---|---|
| C-01 | The system shall be introduced in phases, not as a single cutover. | Stated |
| C-02 | The transition shall not disrupt ongoing resort operations or customer service. | Stated |
| C-03 | Data from the existing local databases shall remain available during and after migration. | Derived |

**Suggested phasing (for stakeholder confirmation):**
1. Centralized database and staff booking system, including data migration and access control.
2. Customer portal for online bookings.
3. Digital financial and payment tracking and reporting.
4. Website redesign, weather forecast, and social media features.

---

## 6. Assumptions

- A1: "Bookings" covers room reservations, class bookings, and hire bookings. The receptionist editing right applies to all three.
- A2: Each Resort Lead may edit only their own resort's data, although they can view all resorts.
- A3: The owner has at least view access to everything. The owner's editing rights were not discussed.
- A4: Telephone bookings will continue alongside online bookings. Fax may be phased out.
- A5: "Payment data" means staff can view payment records and reports. Whether customers pay online was not stated.

---

## 7. Open Issues and Follow-Up Questions

These gaps were not resolved in the interview and should be clarified with Mary Smith:

1. **Online payment:** Should customers pay, or pay a deposit, online through the portal, or only at the resort?
2. **Cancellation and change policy:** What rules apply to customer cancellations, modifications, and refunds?
3. **Owner permissions:** Should the owner have editing rights across all resorts?
4. **Resort Lead scope:** Confirm that leads may edit only their own resort's data.
5. **Reporting:** Beyond payment data, which reports are needed (e.g., occupancy, revenue per resort, inventory usage)?
6. **Phasing order:** Confirm or adjust the suggested phase sequence and timeline.
7. **Existing systems:** What are the details of the current local databases (format, volume) for migration planning?
8. **Social media:** What exactly is expected, e.g., share buttons, embedded feeds, or campaign management?
9. **Weather data:** Is there a preferred forecast provider, and should snow and slope conditions also be shown?
10. **Other stakeholders:** Resort leads, receptionists, and financial assistants were not interviewed. They should be consulted to validate staff workflows.
11. **Budget, timeline, and hosting:** None of these were discussed.
12. **Languages and currencies:** Does the portal need more than English and USD?
