# Software Requirements: Cool Ski Resorts

## 1. Purpose and scope

The system shall digitalize the operations of Cool Ski Resorts, a chain of three ski resorts in the United States. It shall support hotel reservations, ski equipment and clothing hire, ski training class bookings, inventory management, and financial tracking.

The system shall provide a customer-facing website and portal, together with shared operational data accessible from all three resorts. It shall replace or modernize the existing semi-manual processes and local databases through a phased implementation that minimizes disruption.

## 2. Functional requirements

### 2.1 Customer website and portal

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-01 | The system shall provide an online customer portal serving all three resorts. |
| FR-02 | The portal shall allow customers to make hotel room reservations at their chosen resort. |
| FR-03 | The portal shall allow customers to book ski training classes at their chosen resort. |
| FR-04 | The portal shall support customer bookings for ski equipment and clothing hire. The precise hire workflow requires confirmation. |
| FR-05 | The portal shall allow customers to view and manage their reservations. The permitted changes and cancellation rules require clarification. |
| FR-06 | The customer-facing website shall display weather forecasts relevant to the resorts. |
| FR-07 | The system shall support promotion through social media. The required promotional capabilities and platforms require clarification. |

### 2.2 Resort operations

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-08 | The system shall enable staff to digitally record, view, and maintain hotel reservations. |
| FR-09 | The system shall enable staff to digitally record, view, and maintain ski training class bookings. |
| FR-10 | The system shall enable staff to digitally record, view, and maintain equipment and clothing hire records. |
| FR-11 | The system shall maintain inventory records for each resort, including equipment and clothing available for hire. |
| FR-12 | The system shall maintain payment data and support financial tracking for each resort. Online payment processing was not explicitly requested and requires confirmation. |

### 2.3 Shared data and updates

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-13 | The system shall maintain a shared database covering all three resorts. |
| FR-14 | The database shall contain reservations, class bookings, hire records, inventory records, and payment data. |
| FR-15 | Each operational record shall be associated with its relevant resort. |
| FR-16 | Authorized staff at any resort shall be able to view data for all three resorts. |
| FR-17 | Changes to shared data shall be reflected across resort locations through live updates. The maximum acceptable update delay requires definition. |

### 2.4 Access control

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-18 | The system shall distinguish staff users and enforce role-based viewing and editing permissions. |
| FR-19 | All staff users shall have viewing access to shared resort data, subject to confirmation of restrictions on sensitive information. |
| FR-20 | Resort leads shall have permission to edit resort operational data. Whether a lead may edit only their own resort’s data or all resorts’ data requires clarification. |
| FR-21 | Receptionists shall additionally have permission to edit bookings. The booking types covered by this permission require clarification. |
| FR-22 | Staff without resort-lead or receptionist editing permissions shall not be permitted to edit operational records. |

## 3. Non-functional requirements and transition constraints

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| NFR-01 | The customer-facing website shall have an attractive design. Specific design and usability acceptance criteria shall be agreed with the stakeholder. |
| NFR-02 | The system shall support access to shared resort data from each of the three resort locations. |
| NFR-03 | The system shall be introduced through phased implementation. |
| NFR-04 | Implementation shall preserve ongoing resort operations and avoid disruption to reservations, hire services, class bookings, and financial tracking. A measurable continuity target requires agreement. |

## 4. Priorities

The stakeholder identified the following as the highest priorities:

1. Customer portal.
2. Access to data across all resorts.
3. Digitalization of resort operations.

Website presentation, weather forecasts, and social media promotion are additional requested capabilities; their relative priority was not specified.

## 5. Outstanding requirements decisions

The following points must be resolved before detailed design and acceptance testing:

- **Booking rules:** availability checks, amendments, cancellations, refunds, and booking confirmations.
- **Hire workflow:** whether customers reserve items online, request hire, or complete the entire hire transaction.
- **Customer access:** whether accounts are required and how customers retrieve their bookings.
- **Inventory scope:** item categories, stock quantities, availability tracking, and any maintenance tracking.
- **Financial scope:** required payment fields, financial reports, and whether payment collection or accounting integration is needed.
- **Permissions:** cross-resort editing rights, receptionist booking permissions, owner access, and access to sensitive customer or payment data.
- **Live updates:** acceptable latency, concurrent-edit handling, and behavior during connectivity failures.
- **Transition:** migration of existing local databases, treatment of historical records, rollout sequence, and continued handling of telephone and fax reservations.
- **Website enhancements:** forecast locations and sources, social media platforms, and the promotional functions required.
- **Quality and security:** performance, availability, accessibility, privacy, backup, and recovery requirements, none of which were specified in the interview.
