# Software Requirements Specification
## Cool Ski Resorts: Centralized Booking & Resort Management Platform

---

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Purpose
This document defines the software requirements for digitalizing the operations of Cool Ski Resorts, a chain of three ski resorts owned by Mary. It is based on a single elicitation interview with the owner. Some areas still need validation (see Section 9).

### 1.2 Background (Current State)
- Each resort currently has a **static website** that shows services and contact information.
- Each resort has a **simple, standalone reservation system**.
- **Reservations, hires and payments** are handled semi-manually by telephone and fax at each resort. The data is stored in a **local database per resort**.
- The owner manages **accounts and taxation**. Assistants compile the reports for her.
- There are **no formal guidelines** for how reservations, bookings, hires or inventory are handled.

### 1.3 Business Goals
| ID | Goal |
|----|------|
| BG-1 | Streamline business operations across all three resorts. |
| BG-2 | Increase revenue by reaching a larger, US-wide customer base. |
| BG-3 | Become more competitive in the market, including through innovative features. |
| BG-4 | Make financial reporting easier. |
| BG-5 | Achieve the transition without significantly disrupting current operations. |

### 1.4 Scope
**In scope:**
- A public customer-facing website.
- Online reservations, class bookings and equipment hire.
- Customer accounts.
- Online payments.
- Centralized inventory management.
- Financial reporting.
- Weather information.
- Social media promotion support.
- Content maintenance by assistants.

**Out of scope (unless later confirmed):**
- Full accounting or tax filing software.
- Staff recruitment or HR functions.
- Social media login.

### 1.5 Definitions
| Term | Meaning (as interpreted) |
|------|--------------------------|
| Reservation | Reserving a stay or resort service, likely accommodation (*to be confirmed*). |
| Booking | Booking a ski class or lesson. |
| Hire | Rental of ski equipment, not staff hiring (*to be confirmed*). |
| Resort | One of the three Cool Ski Resorts locations. |

### 1.6 Requirement Source Legend
- **[S]** Stated directly by the stakeholder.
- **[C]** Proposed by the interviewer and confirmed by the stakeholder.
- **[D]** Derived by the reviewer from the stated goals or context. These need validation.

### 1.7 Priority Legend
- **Must**: essential for the first release.
- **Should**: important but not critical.
- **Could**: desirable.

| User Class | Description | Main Needs |
|------------|-------------|------------|

## 8. Assumptions and Constraints
- **A-1:** "Hire" means equipment rental, not staff recruitment.
- **A-2:** "Reservations" refers to accommodation or stays, and "bookings" refers to classes. This needs confirmation.
- **A-3:** Customers are mainly based in the US. No multi-language or multi-currency support is needed.
- **A-4:** Interviewer-proposed features (payment gateway and the inventory features) are accepted as requirements because the stakeholder confirmed them.
- **C-1:** The new system must coexist with current operations during transition.
- **C-2:** Only the owner and assistants were identified as stakeholders. Resort staff and customers have not been consulted.

## 9. Open Issues and Questions for Follow-Up
1. **Reservation types:** What exactly can be reserved (lodging, lift passes, ski passes, packages)? Are lift tickets in scope?
2. **"Hiring":** Please confirm this means equipment rental only, and whether staff hiring is relevant.
3. **Guest bookings:** Is registration mandatory, or may customers book as guests?
4. **Policies:** What are the cancellation, refund, deposit and modification rules?
5. **Pricing:** Is pricing dynamic (seasonal or peak) or fixed? Are discounts, packages or promo codes needed?
6. **Reports:** What specific report formats, frequency and tax-related outputs are needed? Is integration with existing accounting software or an accountant required?
7. **Expenses:** How are expenses captured today, and should the system record them or import them?
8. **Inventory scope:** Does inventory cover only rental equipment, or also retail and consumables?
9. **Resort staff:** Who operates the system at each resort? Should they be interviewed?
10. **Weather and social media:** Which providers or platforms are preferred? Is automated posting needed, or are links and sharing enough?
11. **Legacy data:** What format and quality is the data in the existing local databases?
12. **Budget, timeline and hosting:** No constraints were discussed. These need to be defined.
13. **Measurable targets:** Define KPIs for "streamline" and "compete", for example the percentage of bookings made online or the reduction in reporting time.
