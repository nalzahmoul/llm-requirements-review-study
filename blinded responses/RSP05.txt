# Software Requirements Specification (SRS)

## 1. Project Overview
* **Project Name:** Multi-Resort Customer Management & Booking Website
* **Stakeholder:** Ski Resort Owner (manages 3 ski resorts)
* **Objective:** To replace the current manual (phone/fax/in-person) reservation and payment processes with a centralized, intuitive web-based platform. The system will manage bookings, payments, and equipment inventory across all three ski resorts while aiding in customer acquisition.

## 2. Functional Requirements

### 2.1. Reservation and Booking System (High Priority)
* **FR-1.1 (Room Booking):** The system shall allow customers to view availability and book rooms across the three ski resorts.
* **FR-1.2 (Equipment Reservation):** The system shall allow customers to reserve ski equipment.
* **FR-1.3 (Class Registration):** The system shall allow customers to view and sign up for ski training classes.
* **FR-1.4 (Multi-Resort Support):** The system must support operations for 3 distinct ski resort locations (e.g., allowing customers to select which resort they are booking for).

### 2.2. Payment System (High Priority)
* **FR-2.1 (Online Payments):** The system shall process payments online at the time of booking, replacing the current in-resort manual payment process.
* **FR-2.2 (Payment Methods):** The payment gateway shall support major Credit Cards and PayPal.

### 2.3. Inventory Management
* **FR-3.1 (Equipment Tracking):** The system shall include an inventory management module to track available ski equipment.
* **FR-3.2 (Real-Time Updates):** The inventory system shall integrate directly with the booking system to automatically adjust equipment availability in real-time as reservations are made or canceled.

### 2.4. Weather Forecast
* **FR-4.1 (Daily Weather):** The website shall display a daily weather forecast for the resorts to inform customers of current skiing conditions.

### 2.5. Social Media Integration
* **FR-5.1 (Social Sharing & Connectivity):** The website shall integrate with major social media platforms to allow general content sharing and connectivity to drive advertising and customer acquisition.

### 2.6. Out of Scope (Explicitly Excluded)
* **Customer Reviews/Feedback:** The system shall *not* include a customer review or feedback feature, as the stakeholder explicitly rejected this in favor of focusing on advertising.

## 3. Non-Functional Requirements & Architecture

* **NFR-1 (Data Centralization):** The system shall utilize a single, centralized database to store and manage all customer data, reservations, payments, and inventory across all three resorts.
* **NFR-2 (Usability):** The user interface (UI) for the reservation and payment process must be highly intuitive and simple for customers to navigate.
* **NFR-3 (Platform):** The system shall be accessible as a web application (Website).

## 4. Reviewer’s Notes, Risks, and Missing Requirements

As a requirements reviewer, I noticed several gaps in the transcript caused by the stakeholder's brief responses and the interviewer asking "leading" questions (e.g., offering multiple options and getting a single "Yes" in response). **The following points must be clarified in a follow-up session:**

1. **Multi-Resort Architecture:** The stakeholder owns 3 resorts, which was never explored. 
   * *Question:* Will this be one unified website where users select a location via a dropdown, or three separate websites connected to one central database? 
2. **Inventory Scope:** The stakeholder only explicitly mentioned tracking *equipment*. 
   * *Question:* Does the "inventory" system also need to manage room availability and class capacity limits (e.g., max 10 students per ski instructor)?
3. **Admin/Back-Office Portal:** The stakeholder wants to "manage customers," which implies an administrative side to the website.
   * *Question:* What do resort staff need to see? Do they need dashboards to check people in, mark equipment as "returned" or "damaged," or manually enter a phone reservation?
4. **Cancellations and Refunds:** Moving from in-person payments to online payments introduces refund scenarios.
   * *Question:* What is the cancellation policy, and does the system need to handle automated refunds?
5. **Social Media Specifics:** The stakeholder agreed to "general sharing across major platforms" for advertising.
   * *Question:* Which specific platforms (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok)? Does this mean simple "Share" buttons, embedded social feeds, or installing tracking pixels (like the Meta Pixel) for running paid ads?
